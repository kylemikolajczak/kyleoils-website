## Essential Oils: Basics

### 01. What are essential oils?

Essential oils are concentrated aromatic plant extracts. Kyle Oils explains them mainly in the context of scent, conscious everyday routines and responsible use. If you are new, start with the basics and learn a few oils first rather than trying everything at once.

**Link hint:** Knowledge Hub > basics

### 02. How can essential oils be used?

Common approaches include aromatic use, for example in a diffuser, and topical use on the skin, usually with appropriate dilution. Some products also have a specifically labelled internal use. Always follow the individual product label and the directions intended for your market.

**Link hint:** Knowledge Hub > basics / safe use

### 03. What does aromatic use mean?

Aromatic use means consciously experiencing the scent, for example from the bottle or with a diffuser. Less is often more. Room size, ventilation, other people, children and pets should all be considered.

**Link hint:** Diffuser basics / Safe Use

### 04. What does topical use mean?

Topical use means applying an essential oil that is intended for skin use. Because essential oils are highly concentrated, dilution is often appropriate. Consider the product label, skin sensitivity, application area and individual circumstances.

**Link hint:** Dilution

### 05. What is a diffuser?

A diffuser disperses a fine mist of water and essential oils into a room so the aroma can be experienced. For beginners, short and simple routines with small amounts are often enough. Follow the device instructions and the safety information for the oils you use.

**Link hint:** Diffuser basics

### 06. What is a roller?

A roller is a small roll-on bottle in which an essential oil or blend is usually diluted with a carrier oil. It can make a simple, repeatable topical routine easier. The appropriate dilution depends on the product and the person using it.

**Link hint:** Dilution / carrier oils

### 07. Where should I start as a beginner?

Do not begin with as many products as possible. Start with one clear goal and a few simple uses. The Kyle Oils Start Here path guides you through basics, safety, starter kits and first routines step by step. If you are unsure, ask for guidance before buying anything.

**Link hint:** Start Here

## Safe Use

### 08. Are essential oils safe?

Essential oils can be used responsibly in everyday routines, but they are highly concentrated and should not be used casually. Product labels, dilution, amount, method of use and individual circumstances all matter. When in doubt, a cautious approach is better.

**Link hint:** Safe Use

### 09. What does dilution mean with essential oils?

Dilution means mixing an essential oil with a suitable carrier oil before topical use. This can make application more controlled and more appropriate for the skin. The level of dilution depends on factors such as the oil, the person and the application area.

**Link hint:** How to dilute essential oils

### 10. What is a carrier oil?

A carrier oil is a plant-based oil used to dilute essential oils. Fractionated coconut oil is a common example. Carrier oils make topical application easier and help you work with small amounts of essential oil.

**Link hint:** Carrier oils

### 11. What does photosensitivity mean?

Some essential oils can make skin more sensitive to sunlight or UV exposure after topical use. Follow the relevant label and safety instructions carefully. If you are unsure, choose a different approach or ask for guidance.

**Link hint:** Photosensitivity

### 12. Can essential oils be used in the eyes or on mucous membranes?

No. Essential oils should not be placed in the eyes or on sensitive mucous membranes. If accidental contact or a significant reaction occurs, follow the relevant safety guidance and seek appropriate medical advice when needed.

**Link hint:** Eyes / mucous membranes

### 13. What should I consider with sensitive skin?

With sensitive skin, small amounts, appropriate dilution and a cautious start are especially important. A patch test can provide additional guidance, although it cannot rule out every possible reaction. Stop use if burning, significant redness or another unusual reaction occurs.

**Link hint:** Sensitive skin / patch testing

### 14. What should I consider when children are around?

Extra caution is appropriate around children. Age, product label, amount, dilution, room size and method of use all need to be considered. Not every oil or use is appropriate for every age. Seek qualified guidance if you are unsure.

**Link hint:** Children

### 15. What should I consider when pets are around?

Pets can respond differently to aromas and essential oils than people. Consider ventilation, small amounts, the species involved and the animal's ability to leave the area. Do not simply apply human routines to animals, and seek veterinary advice for specific concerns.

**Link hint:** Pets

### 16. What about pregnancy and breastfeeding?

Pregnancy and breastfeeding are situations where a particularly cautious approach is appropriate. Product labels, amount, method of use and individual factors matter. If you are unsure or have medical questions, involve an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

**Link hint:** Pregnancy / breastfeeding

### 17. What if I take medication or have a health condition?

If you take medication, have a health condition or are receiving medical treatment, essential oils should not be viewed as a substitute for that care. Discuss relevant questions with a qualified healthcare professional and follow product labelling.

**Link hint:** Medication / health conditions

## Routines & Everyday Use

### 38. How do I build a simple essential-oil routine?

Start with one specific everyday moment instead of many products at once. A short morning routine, a conscious scent moment or a simple evening routine is easier to repeat than a complicated plan. If it works well, you can build on it later.

**Link hint:** Routines

### 39. How often should I use a diffuser?

There is no single useful duration for every room and every person. Room size, ventilation, the oil, concentration and the people or animals present all matter. Short, intentional periods with good ventilation are generally a better starting point than continuous scenting.

**Link hint:** Diffuser safety

### 40. Can I blend different essential oils?

Yes. Many people combine aromas into simple blends. For beginners, it is useful to work with only a few oils and understand each one individually. The safety guidance for every oil in a blend still applies.

**Link hint:** Routines / blends
