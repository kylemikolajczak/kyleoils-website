# FAQ Implementation Rules

1. Main FAQ remains `/de/faq/` and `/en/faq/`.
2. Knowledge FAQ remains `/de/wissen/faq/` and `/en/knowledge/faq/`.
3. Footer links point to the main FAQ.
4. Target about 50 strong questions per locale after deduplication, not 50 new questions plus all old duplicates.
5. Give a direct answer first, then a short explanation, then a deeper internal link only where useful.
6. Preserve the calm, educational Kyle Oils voice.
7. Avoid disease, cure, treatment and income claims.
8. For children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication, skin reactions and internal use, stay conservative and follow current labels / qualified guidance.
9. CPTG wording must not imply a government or universal independent certification.
10. Resolve real routes from the repository. Never invent slugs.
11. DE and EN should be semantic equivalents, not awkward literal translations.
12. Do not add FAQPage schema merely to chase Google FAQ rich results. Evaluate current structured-data guidance and document the decision.
13. Preserve existing analytics and consent architecture.
