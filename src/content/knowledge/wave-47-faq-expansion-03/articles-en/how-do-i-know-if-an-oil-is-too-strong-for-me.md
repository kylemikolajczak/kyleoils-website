# How do I know if an oil is too strong for me?

An oil can feel too strong for different reasons.

Sometimes the scent is too intense. Sometimes the skin feels sensitive. Sometimes the situation simply does not fit.

## What to be careful with

Pay extra attention to:

- very intense scent
- skin warmth or unpleasant feeling
- sensitive skin areas
- use on the face
- children or pets in the room
- small rooms without ventilation
- strong oils such as spice oils or hot oils

## What to check first

Before using an oil, check:

- current product label
- use context
- dilution guidance
- warnings
- whether it is a strong oil
- whether sunlight after skin contact matters

## Less is often better

Especially at the beginning, less is usually more useful.

You do not need to use a lot of an oil to get to know it.

A calm start helps you notice what fits and what does not.

## When to ask Kyle

Ask before experimenting if:

- you are unsure
- an oil feels very intense
- you have sensitive skin
- children or pets are involved
- you want to combine several products

## Note

Kyle Oils does not replace medical guidance. If skin reactions, discomfort or uncertainty occur, stop use and seek medical advice if needed.
