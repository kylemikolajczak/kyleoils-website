# When is the doTERRA business still too early?

The doTERRA business is usually too early if you do not yet understand the products yourself.

Kyle Oils intentionally separates customer and business.

First, it should be clear whether doTERRA as a product world fits you at all.

## When it is probably too early

Business is probably too early if:

- you do not really know any product yet
- you expect quick money
- you cannot explain the starter kits
- you do not yet have your own routine
- you would pressure people into something
- you need income promises
- you mainly want a discount

## Why customer experience matters first

If you want to guide other people, it helps to first understand:

- how products work
- which questions come up at the beginning
- what safety means
- where the boundaries are
- what real support requires

## A discount is not automatically business

An official customer account or purchase advantage does not automatically mean you have to build a business.

You are allowed to simply stay a customer.

## Note

Kyle Oils does not make income promises and does not replace legal, tax or financial advice. Business topics are general orientation only.
