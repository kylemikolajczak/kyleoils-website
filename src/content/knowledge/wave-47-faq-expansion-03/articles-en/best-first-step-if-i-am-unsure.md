# What is the best first step if I am unsure?

The best first step is usually not an immediate purchase.

A better start is:

1. understand what doTERRA basically is
2. read a few beginner pages
3. collect your most important questions
4. think about which everyday situation actually matters to you
5. then ask Kyle before choosing a kit

Uncertainty is not a problem. It often means you do not want to order blindly.

## Why this makes sense

Essential oils are concentrated products. It is normal not to understand everything at the beginning.

A calm start helps you avoid unnecessary purchases and build more clarity around products, labels and routines.

## When guidance is useful

Guidance is useful when you notice:

- I do not understand the differences between the kits
- I do not know which oils fit my everyday life
- I have questions about children, pets or sensitive skin
- I do not want to click through the shop alone
- I want an honest recommendation

## Note

Kyle Oils does not replace medical guidance and does not make healing promises. The content helps you understand doTERRA products and routines more clearly.
