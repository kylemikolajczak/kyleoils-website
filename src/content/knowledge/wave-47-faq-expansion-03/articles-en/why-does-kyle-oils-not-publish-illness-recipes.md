# Why does Kyle Oils not publish illness recipes?

Because Kyle Oils intentionally does not publish illness-related DIY recipes.

This especially applies to blends that could be presented as solutions for colds, infections, pain, digestion, sleep problems or other complaints.

## Why this boundary matters

Essential oils are concentrated products.

Public illness recipes can quickly become problematic because they may:

- create false expectations
- sound medical
- oversimplify dosage questions
- ignore safety questions
- bypass product labels
- ignore individual situations

## What Kyle does instead

Kyle prefers to explain:

- what a product is
- how to read the label
- which safety questions matter
- which everyday routine is meant
- when personal guidance makes more sense
- when medical guidance comes first

## Does that mean I cannot ask questions?

No.

You can ask questions.

But Kyle Oils does not answer them as a public illness recipe and not as a replacement for medical guidance.

## Note

Kyle Oils does not replace medical guidance and does not make healing promises. For complaints, illness or uncertainty, medical advice should be sought.
