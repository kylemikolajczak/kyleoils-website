# Do I need to know what I want to buy before guidance?

No.

You do not need to know which oil, starter kit or routine fits you before a guidance conversation.

That is exactly what guidance is for.

## What you can bring

It helps if you can roughly say:

- why doTERRA interests you
- whether you already have products
- whether you want to read, smell, start or compare
- whether children, pets or sensitive skin matter
- whether you prefer English or German

You do not need more at the beginning.

## What Kyle does not do

Kyle will not push you into a specific kit.

Good guidance can also lead to:

- start smaller
- wait for now
- read this page first
- this kit may not fit
- you may not need that much yet

## Why this matters

Many people feel overwhelmed in the shop quickly.

Guidance should help bring order to the options, not create more confusion.

## Note

Kyle Oils does not replace medical guidance and does not make healing promises. Guidance is orientation and does not replace reading current product labels.
