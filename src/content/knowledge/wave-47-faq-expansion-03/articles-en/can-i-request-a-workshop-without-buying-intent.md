# Can I request a workshop even without buying intent?

Yes.

A workshop does not mean that everyone has to buy something immediately.

A good beginner workshop can be orientation first.

## When this makes sense

A workshop without direct buying intent can make sense if:

- a small group first wants to understand doTERRA
- questions about use and safety are open
- people are unsure whether oils fit them at all
- a team, studio or practice environment wants to explore the topic
- you do not want a classic sales event

## What should still be clear

A workshop needs a setting.

It helps to clarify beforehand:

- who will attend?
- how many people will be there?
- are they beginners or existing customers?
- which questions matter?
- should it be in English or German?
- should buying be possible later or intentionally not be the focus?

## No pressure

Kyle Oils sees workshops as education and orientation.

If someone does not want to buy afterwards, that is okay.

## Note

Kyle Oils does not replace medical guidance and does not make healing promises. Workshops help people understand products, routines and safety questions more clearly.
