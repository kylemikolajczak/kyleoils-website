# Can I receive guidance in English?

Yes.

Kyle can guide you in English or German.

This is especially helpful if you live in Germany but can express yourself more clearly in English when it comes to products, everyday routines or personal questions.

## Who is this helpful for?

English guidance can fit:

- English speakers in Germany
- expats
- international families
- people who first learned about doTERRA in English
- people who prefer to clarify product questions in English

## What can be clarified in English

In English, Kyle can help you understand:

- what doTERRA is
- which starter kits exist
- how to start safely and calmly
- which questions matter before buying
- how to place first routines
- what makes sense after buying

## Important

The language does not change the approach.

Guidance stays calm, honest and without pressure.

## Note

Kyle Oils does not replace medical guidance and does not make healing promises. English guidance helps you understand products, routines and safety questions more clearly.
