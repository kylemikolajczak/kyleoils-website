# What happens if I have questions after buying?

Then you can ask Kyle.

Support does not end the moment you open a customer account or order a starter kit.

Often, the most important questions come after the purchase.

## Common questions after buying

Many people ask:

- what should I start with first?
- which oils should I leave for later?
- what does this label mean?
- how much scent is useful?
- what matters with skin contact?
- how do I sort my products?
- which routine really fits my everyday life?

## Why after-purchase support matters

A starter kit only makes sense if you understand it.

After unboxing, it is helpful to sort:

- what is clear?
- what is unclear?
- what feels like too much?
- what fits well?
- which safety questions remain?
