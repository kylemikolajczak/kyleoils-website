# Wood oil

A wood oil has a woody, warm or structured scent profile. Examples can include Cedarwood, Cypress or sandalwood-style profiles.

## Why this term matters

Wood oils can give blends depth and make bright citrus notes feel rounder.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Woody does not automatically mean mild. Wood oils should still be tested sparingly and consciously.
