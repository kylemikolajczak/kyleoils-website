# Adulteration

Adulteration means an oil has been altered, diluted or supplemented with unexpected substances. Quality testing is meant to identify such deviations.

## Why this term matters

This term explains why quality testing is important with essential oils.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Even an unadulterated oil still requires safe use. Quality and use safety are two different topics.
