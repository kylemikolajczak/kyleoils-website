# Herbal oil

A herbal oil smells herbal, green, fresh or spicy. Examples include Rosemary, Cilantro, Basil or similar herbal profiles.

## Why this term matters

Herbal oils can make blends feel clearer and more natural.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Herbal oils can be intense. Especially at the beginning, test sparingly and avoid mixing too many new herbal profiles at once.
