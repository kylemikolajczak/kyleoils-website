# Patch test

A patch test is a small tolerance test on an inconspicuous skin area before using a new oil or blend more broadly.

## Why this term matters

A patch test is especially useful for new topical use, sensitive skin or strong oils.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

A patch test is not a medical guarantee. If reaction, burning or uncertainty occurs, stop use and seek professional guidance.
