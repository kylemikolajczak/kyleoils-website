# Hot oil

Hot oil is a common term for especially intense essential oils, often spice or herbal oils, that can feel strong on the skin.

## Why this term matters

The term helps place oils like Oregano, Cinnamon or Clove more cautiously.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Never use hot oils casually. Dilution, labels, small amounts and clear safety boundaries are especially important.
