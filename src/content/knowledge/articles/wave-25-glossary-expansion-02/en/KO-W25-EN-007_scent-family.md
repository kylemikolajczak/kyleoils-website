# Scent family

A scent family describes the direction an oil smells, for example citrusy, woody, floral, herbal, spicy or fresh.

## Why this term matters

Scent families help you choose oils more clearly and blend more intentionally.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

A scent family is an orientation, not a fixed rule. Your own perception still matters.
