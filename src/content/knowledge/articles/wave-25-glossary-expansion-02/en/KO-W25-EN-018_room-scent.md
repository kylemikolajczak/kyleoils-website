# Room scent

Room scent describes the intentionally shaped scent atmosphere in a room. Essential oils can be used for this through diffusers or other suitable methods.

## Why this term matters

Room scent is one of the simplest starting points because you can explore an oil first through scent and atmosphere.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Room scent should stay pleasantly in the background. Not all people or animals enjoy strong scents.
