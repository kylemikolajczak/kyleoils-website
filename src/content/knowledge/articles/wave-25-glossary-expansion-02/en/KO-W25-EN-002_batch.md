# Batch

A batch is a specific production or bottling lot of a product. With essential oils, a batch can help trace quality, sourcing and testing more clearly.

## Why this term matters

This term matters when discussing quality testing, transparency or product standards.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

A batch reference does not replace safe use. Even a tested oil remains concentrated and should be used consciously.
