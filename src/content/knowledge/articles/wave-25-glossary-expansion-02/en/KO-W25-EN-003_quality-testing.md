# Quality testing

Quality testing describes tests and controls used to check whether an oil matches the intended standard. doTERRA describes its CPTG testing process in this context.

## Why this term matters

For visitors, quality testing matters because essential oils are concentrated and trust in the product plays a major role.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Quality matters, but it is not a medical promise and does not replace safety rules.
