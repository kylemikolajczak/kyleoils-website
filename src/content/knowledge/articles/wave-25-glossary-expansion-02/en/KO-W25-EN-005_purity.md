# Purity

Purity in the oil context means a product matches its defined profile as unadulterated as possible and without unwanted additions.

## Why this term matters

Purity is a quality term. It matters when you look at essential oils not only by scent, but by product standard.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Purity is not the same as tolerance. Even pure essential oils can be too intense for skin, children, pets or sensitive people.
