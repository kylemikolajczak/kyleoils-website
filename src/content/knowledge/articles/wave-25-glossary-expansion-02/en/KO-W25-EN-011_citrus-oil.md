# Citrus oil

A citrus oil comes from citrus fruits such as Lemon, Lime, Grapefruit, Bergamot or Wild Orange. These oils often smell bright, fresh and sparkling.

## Why this term matters

Citrus oils are popular for room scent, fresh blends and light everyday routines.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Some citrus oils can be photosensitive when used topically. Always follow the product label and sun or UV guidance.
