# Diffuser blend

A diffuser blend combines several essential oils for a specific room-scent idea.

## Why this term matters

Diffuser blends help shape atmosphere more intentionally: fresh, warm, forest-like, soft or clear.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Do not mix too many oils and do not diffuse intensely for long periods. Consider room size, ventilation, children, pets and scent sensitivity.
