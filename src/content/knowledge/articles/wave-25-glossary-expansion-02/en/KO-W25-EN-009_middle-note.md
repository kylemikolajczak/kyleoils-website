# Middle note

A middle note is a scent part that often carries the character of a blend. It sits between the first impression and the deeper base notes.

## Why this term matters

Middle notes help build blends that feel more rounded and understandable.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

This is scent logic, not an emotional or physical promise.
