# Conifer oil

A conifer oil comes from conifer profiles such as Fir, Spruce or Pine. It often smells forest-like, resinous, green and clear.

## Why this term matters

Conifer oils fit forest atmospheres, fresh room-scent blends and natural base notes.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Conifer oils can be quite present. In small rooms, around children, pets or sensitive people, start sparingly.
