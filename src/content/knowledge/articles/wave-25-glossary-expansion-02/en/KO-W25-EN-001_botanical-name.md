# Botanical name

The botanical name is the scientific plant name of an oil, for example Citrus limon for Lemon. It helps distinguish plants clearly, even when everyday names sound similar.

## Why this term matters

On Kyle Oils, the botanical name helps you place an oil more precisely and avoid confusion between similar plant profiles.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

The botanical name does not tell you whether a use is right for you personally. Labels, dilution, context and safety still matter.
