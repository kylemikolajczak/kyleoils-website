# Floral oil

A floral oil has a floral scent profile. It can feel soft, sweet, rounded, elegant or very intense.

## Why this term matters

Floral oils can make blends feel warmer, softer or more personal.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Floral scents can quickly dominate. A few drops or small portions are often enough.
