# Base note

A base note is a deeper scent part often perceived for longer. Wood, resin and some conifer profiles can feel base-note-like.

## Why this term matters

Base notes give blends depth, calm and structure.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Deep or long-lasting does not automatically mean better. In small rooms, base notes can quickly become too present.
