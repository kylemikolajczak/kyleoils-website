# PV

PV stands for Product Volume in the doTERRA context. The value is used in certain doTERRA programs, orders or qualifications.

## Why this term matters

PV is mainly relevant when discussing loyalty orders, programs or business structures.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

PV should not be the reason to buy products you do not understand or use. Routine first, structure second.
