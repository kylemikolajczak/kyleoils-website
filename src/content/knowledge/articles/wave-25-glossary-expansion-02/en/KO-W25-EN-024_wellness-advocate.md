# Wellness Advocate

Wellness Advocate is a doTERRA term for people who can recommend doTERRA products, support customers or build a doTERRA business.

## Why this term matters

On Kyle Oils, the focus is calm guidance: understanding, safe context and fitting routines.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

The term should not be connected with income promises or pressure. Good guidance remains honest and voluntary.
