# Roll-on

A roll-on is a product or blend in a small bottle with a rollerball top. It is usually used for simple, targeted topical application.

## Why this term matters

Roll-ons can be practical because they are easy to keep nearby and often fit routines well.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Roll-ons still require safe use: follow the label, avoid eyes and mucous membranes and test carefully on sensitive skin.
