# Top note

A top note is a scent part often noticed first and often feels lighter or more volatile. Many citrus oils are described as top-note-like.

## Why this term matters

The term helps explain blends, especially when a scent starts bright and later becomes calmer.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Top notes are not effect levels. This is about scent perception, not medical effects.
