# CPTG

CPTG stands for Certified Pure Tested Grade at doTERRA. doTERRA uses this term for its testing process around purity, composition and possible adulteration.

## Why this term matters

The term helps explain doTERRA’s quality position, especially when you compare products.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

CPTG does not mean every use is automatically appropriate. Product labels, dilution and personal context still matter.
