# Spice oil

A spice oil has a warm, spicy or sharp scent profile. Examples can include Cinnamon, Clove or similar spice profiles.

## Why this term matters

Spice oils can give blends warmth and character, but should be used very intentionally.

## Kyle Oils context

Kyle Oils explains this term simply so you can understand product texts, oil profiles, safety pages and doTERRA language more easily. The glossary does not replace personal guidance, but it helps you avoid getting lost in terminology.

## Important note

Many spice oils are considered strong. For topical use, take dilution, labels and safety context especially seriously.
