# Introductory Kit or enrolment kit?

The difference between a small introductory kit and an enrolment kit, explained calmly.

## Focus

small start, enrolment, kit size, customer account

## Two different starting ideas

A small introductory kit may fit when you first want to smell, test and understand. An enrolment kit may make sense when you want to start directly with an account, several products and a clearer entry into the doTERRA world.

## What doTERRA shows officially

doTERRA lists both introductory products and enrolment kits in the shop. According to doTERRA, enrolment kits include the enrolment fee to start the doTERRA journey.

## Why size is not everything

A large kit is not automatically better. A small kit is not automatically too little. What matters is whether you can actually use and safely place the included products.

## When Kyle can help

Kyle can help you check whether you want a small introduction first or whether an enrolment kit makes more sense because of product range, account and longer-term use.

## In short

Introductory Kit and enrolment kit are two different paths. The right choice depends on your everyday life, not on pressure.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure which starting path fits, ask Kyle. The goal is not to push you into the biggest kit or the fastest decision. The goal is a start you understand, use safely and feel good about long term.
