# LRP loyalty program explained calmly

What doTERRA LRP basically is, when it may make sense and why it is not mandatory for getting started.

## Focus

LRP, loyalty program, reorder, product points

## What LRP basically is

LRP stands for Loyalty Rewards Program. It is doTERRA’s loyalty program for recurring orders and product experience over time.

## Why it makes sense for some people

LRP can become interesting when you use products regularly, build routines or work with doTERRA long term. It fits less when you only want to test something once.

## Why LRP is not required at the start

You do not have to start with LRP just because you want to get to know doTERRA. First, it should be clear whether you truly use the products and which routines fit you.

## What Kyle clarifies with you

Kyle can help you see LRP not as a pressure system, but as a possible structure. When does it make sense? Which products do you use regularly? What would be too much?

## In short

LRP is designed for repetition and longer-term use. For first contact, it can calmly come later.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure which starting path fits, ask Kyle. The goal is not to push you into the biggest kit or the fastest decision. The goal is a start you understand, use safely and feel good about long term.
