# Which doTERRA starter kit fits me?

A calm decision guide for your first doTERRA kit, without overwhelm or purchase pressure.

## Focus

Starter kit choice, everyday use, budget, guidance

## Why the kit question matters

Many people do not start doTERRA with a single oil, but with a kit. That can make sense because you can cover several everyday situations right away. It can also feel overwhelming when you do not know which oils truly fit you.

## The better starting question

Do not ask first: Which kit is the biggest? Ask instead: What do you actually want to use in everyday life? Is it evening routine, family, scent, body care, digestive feel, seasons, kitchen logic or simply a clean first introduction?

## What Kyle looks at with you

Kyle looks with you at your everyday situation, scent preferences, budget, possible children or pets in the home, safety questions and whether a smaller start or a larger kit makes more sense.

## Why guidance before buying helps

A kit should not sit unused in a cupboard. It should be understandable. When you know before buying what each oil is for, the start feels calmer and less random.

## In short

The best starter kit is not automatically the biggest one. It is the kit you understand, can use safely and truly bring into your everyday life.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure which starting path fits, ask Kyle. The goal is not to push you into the biggest kit or the fastest decision. The goal is a start you understand, use safely and feel good about long term.
