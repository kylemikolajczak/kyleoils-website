# Wholesale Customer or Wellness Advocate?

The two doTERRA account types explained calmly: buy with benefits or also become active in business.

## Focus

account types, Wholesale Customer, Wellness Advocate, partner start

## Why the account type matters

doTERRA distinguishes between people who mainly want to use products and people who also want to share, guide or become active in business.

## What doTERRA describes officially

In the European enrolment context, doTERRA describes two membership types: Wholesale Customer and Wellness Advocate. The Wholesale Customer is more focused on product use and shopping. The Wellness Advocate can additionally relate to business and team building.

## No pressure toward business

Not every customer needs to become an advisor. For many people, a customer account is enough. If interest develops later, the possibility can be explored calmly.

## When Wellness Advocate becomes interesting

Wellness Advocate can become interesting when you do not only want to use products, but also recommend them, guide people or build a doTERRA activity. No income promises, no hype.

## In short

The right account type depends on whether you only want to buy and use, or whether you may also want to share and build later.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure which starting path fits, ask Kyle. The goal is not to push you into the biggest kit or the fastest decision. The goal is a start you understand, use safely and feel good about long term.
