# Week 4: review, clarify questions, force nothing

How to honestly review what fits, what may wait and which questions remain open after the first weeks.

## Focus

review, questions, guidance, next steps

## Why week 4 matters

After a few weeks, you have collected real experience. You know better which scents you like, which products you use and where you still feel unsure.

## What you can review

Which routine did you repeat? Which product stays unused? Where was the scent too strong? Where do you have safety questions? What would you like to understand better?

## Not everything has to fit

It is normal if not every oil becomes your favorite right away. Some things fit later, some only rarely, some perhaps not at all.

## The check with Kyle

Now is a good moment to review with Kyle. Together, you can clarify what you continue using, what you leave out and whether a next step makes sense.

## No pressure toward buying or business

After 30 days, you do not have to buy more or become an advisor. If interest develops, it can be placed calmly.

## In short

Week 4 is not the end. It is the moment where trying things becomes a calmer routine.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you feel unsure after getting started, you do not have to experiment alone. Kyle helps you calmly sort your products, routines and safety questions.

This page does not replace official doTERRA product labels, safety information or personal professional guidance. Always read the current product information and ask when unsure.
