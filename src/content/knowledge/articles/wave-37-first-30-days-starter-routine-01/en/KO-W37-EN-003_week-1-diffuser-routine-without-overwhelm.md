# Week 1: diffuser routine without overwhelm

A careful start with room scent and diffuser routines, without too much scent at once.

## Focus

diffuser, room scent, amount, ventilation

## Why the diffuser can be an easy start

Aromatic use is the easiest first contact for many people. You smell an oil, notice your preferences and do not have to start topically or internally right away.

## Start small

Begin with little scent, a short run time and a well-ventilated room. The goal is not to fill the room, but to find out what feels pleasant.

## One oil or a very simple blend

At the beginning, use one oil or a simple blend. That helps you learn what you actually like.

## Consider children and pets

If children or pets are in the home, start especially carefully. The room should be open and pets should be able to leave.

## What to observe

Is the scent pleasant? Does it become too strong? Do you want to ventilate? Would you use it again? This observation matters more than a perfect recipe.

## In short

A good diffuser routine is not strong. It is pleasant, clear and conscious.
