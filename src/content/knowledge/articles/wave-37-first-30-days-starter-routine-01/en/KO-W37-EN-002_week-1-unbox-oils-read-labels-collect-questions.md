# Week 1: unbox oils, read labels, collect questions

How to get to know your first doTERRA products calmly in the first week.

## Focus

unboxing, labels, first orientation

## The first moment

When your oils arrive, you do not have to use everything immediately. The first step is orientation.

## What you can do first

Place all products in front of you. Read the product names. Look at the label, usage directions, warnings and whether an oil is mainly aromatic, topical or placed differently.

## Three simple groups

Sort into three groups: I understand this right away. This interests me. I have questions about this.

## Why this matters

Many mistakes do not happen because of bad intent, but because of trying things too quickly. Strong oils, blends, capsules, children, pets or topical use need more calm.

## What you can ask Kyle

Ask Kyle which oil makes sense first, which products you should use later and which safety questions should be clarified directly.

## In short

Week 1 is not an application challenge. Week 1 is orientation.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you feel unsure after getting started, you do not have to experiment alone. Kyle helps you calmly sort your products, routines and safety questions.

## Note

This page does not replace official doTERRA product labels, safety information or personal professional guidance. Always read the current product information and ask when unsure.
