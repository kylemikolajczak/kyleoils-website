# Week 2: build an evening routine with oils

How to build a simple evening routine with scent, room and repetition, without sleep promises.

## Focus

evening routine, scent anchor, repetition

## Why evening is a good area

Evening routine is a natural starting point for many people because scent, room and repetition work well together.

## No sleep promise

An evening routine is not a treatment for sleep problems. It is a conscious transition from day into evening.

## The simple flow

Choose a fixed moment: after tidying, before reading, while winding down or when preparing the bedroom. Use little scent and repeat the same routine for a few days.

## Fitting oil profiles

Lavender, Serenity or soft, warm scent profiles can fit this context. What matters is not what is popular in theory, but what stays pleasant for you.

## What to observe

Does the routine feel calm? Is the scent pleasant? Does it become too much? Would you leave something out?

## In short

An evening routine does not live from strength. It lives from clarity and repetition.
