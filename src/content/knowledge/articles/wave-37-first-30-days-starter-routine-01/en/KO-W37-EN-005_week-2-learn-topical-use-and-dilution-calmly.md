# Week 2: learn topical use and dilution calmly

A careful introduction to topical use, carrier oil, patch testing and sensitive skin.

## Focus

topical use, dilution, carrier oil, skin

## Why topical use needs more attention

Topical use feels practical, but it needs more safety context than simply smelling an oil. Essential oils are concentrated.

## Take dilution seriously

A carrier oil is not a sign of weakness. Dilution can make use more pleasant, reduce skin sensitivity and make the routine more conscious.

## Patch test instead of a courage test

Test new oils slowly and on a small area. Wait and see how your skin responds. If irritation occurs, stop, clean the area and do not keep experimenting.

## Recognize strong oils

Hot oils, spicy oils, citrus oils, Oregano, Cinnamon Bark or intense blends need especially clear context.

## What you can ask Kyle

Ask Kyle which oils you can test topically with care, which ones you should dilute and which ones you should first get to know aromatically.

## In short

Topical use is not a competition. Slow, diluted and conscious is usually the better direction.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you feel unsure after getting started, you do not have to experiment alone. Kyle helps you calmly sort your products, routines and safety questions.
