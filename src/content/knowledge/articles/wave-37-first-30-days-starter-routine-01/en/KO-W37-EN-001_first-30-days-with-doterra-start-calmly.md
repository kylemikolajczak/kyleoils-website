# Your First 30 Days with doTERRA: A Clear Place to Begin

A calm 30-day start plan for new doTERRA customers, without overwhelm and without trying to do everything at once.

## Focus

30 days, getting started, orientation, first routines

## Why a 30-day plan helps

After the first order, the biggest risk is not that you have too few products. The biggest risk is that you try to understand too much at once. A calm 30-day plan helps you avoid doing everything at the same time.

## The basic idea

In the first 30 days, the goal is not to know every oil perfectly. The goal is to start safely, test simple routines, learn your scent preferences and collect questions.

## Week 1: arrive and understand

Open your products calmly. Read the labels. Sort them by scent, use and questions. Start aromatically and carefully before adding more complex uses.

## Week 2: choose one simple routine

Choose one everyday situation: evening, room scent, body care, kitchen logic, digestive feel or seasons. Stay with one routine instead of trying something new every day.

## Week 3: deepen safety

Look at dilution, children, pets, skin reactions, photosensitivity and storage. Safety is not an extra. It is part of the routine.

## Week 4: sort things with Kyle

After a few weeks, you know better what you like, what you use and where you feel unsure. That is exactly when a short check with Kyle becomes useful.

## In short

The first 30 days do not have to be perfect. They should feel calm, safe and understandable.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you feel unsure after getting started, you do not have to experiment alone. Kyle helps you calmly sort your products, routines and safety questions.
