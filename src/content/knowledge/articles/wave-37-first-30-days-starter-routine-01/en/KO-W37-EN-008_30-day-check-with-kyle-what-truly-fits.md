# 30-day check with Kyle: what truly fits?

A calm check after getting started to place products, routines, safety and next steps.

## Focus

30-day check, guidance, next steps

## Why a check makes sense

After getting started, the best questions are often practical, not theoretical. What did you actually use? What felt pleasant? What was too much? What is still unclear?

## What Kyle looks at with you

Kyle can sort products, routines, use, safety, scent preferences, open questions and possible next steps with you.

## Good questions to ask

Which three products do I actually use? What should I not use yet? Which routine fits my everyday life? Do I need to reorder anything? Does LRP make sense at all? Am I interested in going deeper?

## What does not have to happen

You do not automatically have to buy a new kit, order monthly or become an advisor. The check is orientation first.

## Why this helps long term

When you review early, your products stay more understandable. That lowers the chance that they disappear into a cupboard.

## In short

The 30-day check turns a purchase into a better product experience.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you feel unsure after getting started, you do not have to experiment alone. Kyle helps you calmly sort your products, routines and safety questions.
