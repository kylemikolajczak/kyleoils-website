# Week 3: find your three core routines

How to turn many oil ideas into three simple everyday routines.

## Focus

core routines, everyday life, simplicity

## Why three routines are enough

At the beginning, you do not need ten uses. Three simple core routines are enough to develop a feel for your products.

## Possible core areas

Good starting areas are: a room-scent routine, an evening routine and a body-care or massage routine. Alternatively, a food-routine or digestive-feel topic can be added.

## What you may leave out

Anything that confuses you, creates pressure or leaves safety questions open may wait.

## How to choose

Choose routines that already have space in your everyday life. Oils should not add more complexity, but make existing moments more conscious.

## The simple test

A routine is good when you can explain it, use it safely and repeat it for several days.

## In short

Your first routines should be easy enough that you actually use them.

If you feel unsure after getting started, you do not have to experiment alone. Kyle helps you calmly sort your products, routines and safety questions.
