# Kitchen after cooking: reset the scent

How to make a kitchen feel lighter after cooking with air, tidying and fresh scent profiles.

## Focus

kitchen, cooking, fresh scent, citrus and herbs

## Why the kitchen is different

Kitchen scent is often intense: food, heat, spices, steam and surfaces all come together. Essential oils should not cover everything up, but gently reset the room after cooking.

## Ventilate and tidy first

Open a window, clear away obvious kitchen leftovers and clean according to your normal household routine. Scent does not replace cleaning.

## Fresh scent profiles

Lemon, Lime, Green Mandarin, Rosemary, Basil or Spearmint can fit depending on the mood. Citrus feels fresh, herbs feel kitchen-close.

## No cleaning or disinfecting claims

This routine is a scent and room routine. It does not promise disinfection and does not replace suitable household products.

## Careful with surfaces and food

Do not casually place oils on worktops, wood, plastic or food. Read the product label and consider material compatibility.

## In short

After cooking, good scent starts with air, tidying and restraint.
