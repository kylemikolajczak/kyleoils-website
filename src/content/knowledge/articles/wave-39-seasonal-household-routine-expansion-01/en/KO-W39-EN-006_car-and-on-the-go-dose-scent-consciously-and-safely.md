# Car and on the go: dose scent consciously and safely

How to place scent in the car or on the go very carefully, without distraction or overuse.

## Focus

car, on the go, safety, little scent

## Why the car is different

A car is a small, closed space. Scent becomes intense quickly and can distract or feel unpleasant.

## Safety first

While driving, nothing should drip, slide, glare, distract or smell too strong. Scent must never be more important than road safety.

## Less is better

Use, if at all, very little scent and ventilate well. Strong oils, spice oils or heavy resin notes are often too much in the car.

## Consider passengers

Children, pets, pregnant people, sensitive people or people with scent sensitivity should matter more than your scent preference.

## Fitting direction

Fresh, clear and mild profiles can fit better than heavy blends. Still, restraint is the most important rule.

## In short

Good scent in the car is almost invisible. As soon as it stands out or bothers someone, it is too much.
