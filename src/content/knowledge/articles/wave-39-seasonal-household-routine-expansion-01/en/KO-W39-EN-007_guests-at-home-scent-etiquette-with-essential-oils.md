# Guests at home: scent etiquette with essential oils

Why scent around guests should be subtle, welcoming and considerate.

## Focus

guests, visitors, scent etiquette, consideration

## Why scent etiquette matters

What feels pleasant to you can feel strong, unfamiliar or unpleasant to someone else. Around guests, consideration is part of good scent culture.

## Ventilate before guests arrive

Start with fresh air and order. A home often feels fresher through air and clarity, not through more oil.

## Subtle, not impressive

If you use scent, keep it mild, short and in the background. Guests should feel welcomed, not scented.

## Respect sensitivity

With closer guests, it is fine to ask whether scent is okay. Around babies, children, pets, pregnancy or scent sensitivity, stay more restrained.

## Fitting scent profiles

Soft citrus, woody or green profiles can feel friendly. Heavy, spicy or very sweet blends should be used carefully.

## In short

Good scent etiquette means: the person matters more than the scent.
