# Laundry, closet and textiles: use scent with distance

How to place scent around laundry and closet consciously, without putting oils directly onto textiles or skin-contact surfaces.

## Focus

laundry, closet, textiles, skin contact, material care

## Why textiles are sensitive

Textiles touch skin, hold scent strongly and can keep stains. That is why scent around laundry should be placed especially carefully.

## Do not put oils directly on clothing

Do not casually place essential oils directly onto clothing, bedding, towels or fabrics. Some oils can stain or become too intense.

## Scent with distance

An indirect scent moment is better: room scent in the room, an open closet area with distance or a separate scent carrier that does not touch clothing.

## Consider skin contact

Anything that touches skin directly should not be scented uncontrollably. Think of sensitive skin, children and pets.

## Fitting scent profiles

Lavender, Cedarwood, Petitgrain or soft woody notes can fit calm closet or bedroom context, but always subtly.

## In short

With textiles, distance, air and little scent are better than direct oil marks on fabric.
