# Bathroom and guest bathroom: fresh without too much scent

A calm routine for bathroom and guest bathroom where a fresh impression matters more than strong scent.

## Focus

bathroom, guest bathroom, freshness, subtle scent

## Why bathroom scent can quickly be too much

Small rooms hold scent more intensely. A bathroom usually needs less oil, shorter scent moments and more ventilation.

## The basic routine

Ventilate, keep visible surfaces tidy, check towels and only then add a very subtle scent impulse.

## Fitting scent profiles

Lemon, Eucalyptus, Spearmint, Tea Tree or Petitgrain can feel fresh. Use them sparingly and not all together.

## Think of guests

Not every guest likes essential oils. A subtle, short scent is more welcoming than a strongly scented small room.

## No hygiene promises

This routine creates a fresh impression. It does not replace cleaning and does not promise hygienic effects.

## In short

A good guest bathroom does not smell strong. It feels clean, light and pleasant.
