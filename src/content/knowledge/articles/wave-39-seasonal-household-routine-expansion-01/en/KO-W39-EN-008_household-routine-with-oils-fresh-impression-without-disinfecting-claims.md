# Household routine with oils: fresh impression without disinfecting claims

How to use essential oils in the home as scent and ritual support, without cleaning or disinfecting promises.

## Focus

household, freshness, routine, no disinfecting claims

## Why clear boundaries matter

Essential oils are often connected with household cleaning. On a public website, it should be clear: scent is not the same as tested cleaning or disinfection.

## What oils can do well

They can make a household routine feel more pleasant, support a fresh impression and create a small ritual moment.

## What they do not replace

They do not replace suitable cleaning products, hygiene products or instructions on household products.

## Practical placement

After tidying and cleaning, a short room-scent moment with Lemon, Tea Tree, Eucalyptus, Spearmint or Petitgrain can feel pleasant. Use little and ventilate well.

## Safety and materials

Do not put oils uncontrollably onto surfaces. Some materials, coatings, plastics or textiles can react sensitively.

## In short

Household scent may feel fresh. But it should not be sold as disinfection.
