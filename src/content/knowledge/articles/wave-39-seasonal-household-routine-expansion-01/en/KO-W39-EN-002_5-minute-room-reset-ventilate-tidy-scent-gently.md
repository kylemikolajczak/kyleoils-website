# 5-minute room reset: ventilate, tidy, scent gently

A simple everyday routine to make a room feel fresh and clear without much effort.

## Focus

room reset, ventilation, tidying, gentle scent

## Why a room reset helps

Sometimes a room does not need a big cleaning session, but a short conscious restart. Ventilation, tidying surfaces and a gentle scent can change the impression quickly.

## Step 1: air first

Open a window briefly. Scent should not replace fresh air, but complement it.

## Step 2: tidy visibly

Put away two or three visible things. A clearer room lets scent feel calmer.

## Step 3: very little scent

Use a mild diffuser moment or a simple room-scent impulse. Less is better than an overloaded scent.

## Fitting scent profiles

Lemon, Wild Orange, Spearmint, Petitgrain or soft woody notes can fit depending on the room. What matters is that the scent stays pleasant.

## In short

The best room reset is simple: fresh air, less visual chaos, light scent.
