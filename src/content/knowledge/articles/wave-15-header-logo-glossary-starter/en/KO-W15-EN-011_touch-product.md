# Touch product

## Short definition

A Touch product is usually a pre-diluted roll-on product for topical use.

## Why this term matters

Touch products can be practical for beginners because application feels simpler and more controlled.

## Beginner note

Even pre-diluted products require label awareness and should not be used near eyes, mucous membranes or sensitive areas.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
