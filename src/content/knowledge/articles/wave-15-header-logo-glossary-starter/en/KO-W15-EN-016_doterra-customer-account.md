# doTERRA customer account

## Short definition

A doTERRA customer account is an account used to order products and, depending on the market, access purchasing advantages.

## Why this term matters

The account is a practical ordering access point, but it does not replace product orientation or safe use.

## Beginner note

A customer account does not automatically mean you need to sell. Guidance before the first order remains useful.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
