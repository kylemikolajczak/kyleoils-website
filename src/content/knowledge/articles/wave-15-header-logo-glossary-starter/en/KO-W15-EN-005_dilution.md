# Dilution

## Short definition

Dilution means mixing an essential oil with a carrier oil before applying it to the skin.

## Why this term matters

Dilution is one of the most important safety topics for topical use. It helps make skin application more controlled and gentle.

## Beginner note

Dilution is especially important around children, sensitive skin, strong oils and unfamiliar products.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
