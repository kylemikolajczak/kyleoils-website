# Carrier oil

## Short definition

A carrier oil is a fatty plant oil that can be used to dilute essential oils for topical application.

## Why this term matters

Carrier oils help spread essential oils more evenly and make topical routines gentler.

## Beginner note

Examples include fractionated coconut oil, jojoba oil, almond oil and grapeseed oil. Carrier oils can also have personal tolerance considerations.
