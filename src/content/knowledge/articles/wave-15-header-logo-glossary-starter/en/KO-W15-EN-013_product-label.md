# Product label

## Short definition

The product label contains important information about intended use, warnings, ingredients and safe handling.

## Why this term matters

The label is more important than a general internet tip because it refers to the specific product.

## Beginner note

Labels matter especially around children, pets, internal use, topical use and photosensitivity.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
