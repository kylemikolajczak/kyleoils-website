# Internal use

## Short definition

Internal use means taking a product into the body. With essential oils, this is a particularly sensitive area.

## Why this term matters

Not every oil and not every product is intended for internal use. Product labels, health context and qualified guidance are essential.

## Beginner note

For beginners, internal use is not a useful first step. Understand basics, diffuser use and dilution first.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
