# Essential oil blend

## Short definition

An essential oil blend combines several essential oils in one product or in a self-made mixture.

## Why this term matters

Blends can be practical because they provide a finished scent profile or routine idea.

## Beginner note

A blend is not automatically gentler or safer. Labels, dilution and personal tolerance still matter.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
