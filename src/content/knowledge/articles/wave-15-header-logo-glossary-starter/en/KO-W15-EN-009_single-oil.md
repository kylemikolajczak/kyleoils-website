# Single oil

## Short definition

A single oil is an essential oil from one plant or a clearly named plant source.

## Why this term matters

Single oils help beginners understand scent profiles and use guidance step by step.

## Beginner note

Examples include Lavender, Lemon, Peppermint, Frankincense and Tea Tree.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
