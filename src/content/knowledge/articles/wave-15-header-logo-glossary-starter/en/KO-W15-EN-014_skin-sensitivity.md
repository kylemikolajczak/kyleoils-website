# Skin sensitivity

## Short definition

Skin sensitivity means that skin may react sensitively to products, scents or essential oils.

## Why this term matters

Skin sensitivity is one reason why dilution, patch testing and a cautious start matter.

## Beginner note

If redness, burning, itching or discomfort occurs, stop use and seek qualified guidance if needed.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
