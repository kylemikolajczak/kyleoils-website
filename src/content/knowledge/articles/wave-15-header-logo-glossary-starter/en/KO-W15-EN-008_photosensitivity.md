# Photosensitivity

## Short definition

Photosensitivity means that skin may react more strongly to sunlight or UV light after contact with certain substances.

## Why this term matters

With some citrus oils, photosensitivity is an important safety topic, especially when applied to skin before sun exposure.

## Beginner note

Always read the product label and avoid applying to sun-exposed skin when unsure.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
