# Essential oil

## Short definition

An essential oil is a concentrated aromatic plant extract. It contains volatile scent components from a plant and should be used consciously, sparingly and safely.

## Why this term matters

This term is the foundation of the whole Knowledge Hub. When you understand that essential oils are concentrated, you are more likely to use them carefully and realistically.

## Beginner note

For beginners, it is useful to first experience an oil through scent and simple routines before moving into more complex uses.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
