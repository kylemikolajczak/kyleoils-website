# Scent profile

## Short definition

A scent profile describes how an oil or blend smells, for example fresh, soft, herbal, woody, floral or intense.

## Why this term matters

Scent profiles help explain oils without medical promises and make it easier to choose everyday routines.

## Beginner note

What feels pleasant to one person can be too intense for another. Scent remains personal.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
