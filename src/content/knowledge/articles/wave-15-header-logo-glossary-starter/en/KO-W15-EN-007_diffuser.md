# Diffuser

## Short definition

A diffuser is a device that disperses essential oils with water or air so the scent becomes noticeable in a room.

## Why this term matters

For many people, the diffuser is the simplest way to experience essential oils in everyday life.

## Beginner note

Fewer drops, shorter runtime and good ventilation are especially useful for beginners.
