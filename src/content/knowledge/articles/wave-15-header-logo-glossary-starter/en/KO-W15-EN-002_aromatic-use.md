# Aromatic use

## Short definition

Aromatic use means experiencing essential oils through scent, for example in a diffuser or by smelling an oil consciously.

## Why this term matters

For many beginners, aromatic use is the easiest starting point because it does not require direct skin contact.

## Beginner note

Scent still needs consideration: room size, runtime, children, pets and scent sensitivity all matter.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
