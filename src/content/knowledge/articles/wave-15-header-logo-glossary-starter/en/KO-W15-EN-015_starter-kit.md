# Starter kit

## Short definition

A starter kit is a curated product selection that can make starting with doTERRA easier.

## Why this term matters

Starter kits can provide orientation, but they are not automatically the right first step for every person.

## Beginner note

More important than the size of the kit is whether you understand what it includes and what you will actually use.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
