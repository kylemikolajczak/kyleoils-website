# Topical use

## Short definition

Topical use means applying an essential oil externally to the skin, usually diluted with a carrier oil.

## Why this term matters

Skin application is more sensitive than room scent. Dilution, skin response and product labels matter here.

## Beginner note

For beginners: learn dilution first, test carefully and avoid applying several new oils at once.

## Safe context

This glossary explains terms in a simple and everyday-friendly way. It does not replace medical, veterinary or other qualified professional guidance.

Extra caution matters around health questions, children, pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication or sensitive skin.
