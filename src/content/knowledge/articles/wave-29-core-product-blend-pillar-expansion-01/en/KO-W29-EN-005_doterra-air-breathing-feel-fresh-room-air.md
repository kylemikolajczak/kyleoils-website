# doTERRA Air: breathing feel & fresh room air

doTERRA Air belongs to the product areas where people have very concrete expectations. That is exactly why public language needs the right balance: close enough to real everyday use, but without medical or exaggerated claims.

Breathing feel & fresh room air

## Safe public positioning

This page may place doTERRA Air like this:

- for clear, minty room-scent profiles
- for conscious diffuser routines in well-ventilated rooms
- for fresh room air in everyday life and seasonal contexts
- for people who want to separate doTERRA Air safely from medical respiratory language

## What we do not write publicly

These statements do not belong on the website:

- opens airways
- helps coughs or colds
- treats asthma
- helps respiratory disease

## How Kyle places this in guidance

Personal guidance is not about quickly selling a product as a solution. It is about understanding your everyday life:

- What are you really looking for?
- Is this about scent, care, massage, room, food routines or family?
- Which products do you already have?
- What does the product label allow?
- Are children, pets, pregnancy, medication or sensitive skin involved?
- Is a finished doTERRA product more appropriate than a DIY recipe?

This keeps the recommendation closer to what doTERRA actually offers, but without risky online promises.

## Safety

Minty and eucalyptus-like scent profiles can be intense. In small rooms, around children, pets or scent-sensitive people, start sparingly and briefly.
