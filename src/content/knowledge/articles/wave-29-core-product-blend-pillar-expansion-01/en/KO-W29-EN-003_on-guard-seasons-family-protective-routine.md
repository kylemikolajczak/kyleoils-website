# On Guard: seasons, family & protective routine

doTERRA On Guard belongs to the product areas where people have very concrete expectations. That is exactly why public language needs the right balance: close enough to real everyday use, but without medical or exaggerated claims.

## Core area

Family everyday life & safe use

## What doTERRA officially points toward

doTERRA describes On Guard as the Protective Blend and offers an On Guard product line. For Kyle Oils, the focus is seasons, household, scent profile, product choice and safe use.

Kyle Oils uses this area through a clean guidance logic: What is the product? Which everyday situation is it searched for? Which safe-use context matters? Where does personal guidance begin?

## Safe public positioning

This page may place doTERRA On Guard like this:

- as a scent and product area around seasons and family everyday life
- for household routines, hand products and product choice with doTERRA On Guard
- for guidance on which product form fits better: oil, drops, softgels, beadlets, soap or other variants
- as an example of why product form and label matter more than internet recipes

## What we do not write publicly

These statements do not belong on the website:

- fights off illness
- helps when you get sick
- replaces hygiene or medical help
- protects against infections

## How Kyle places this in guidance

Personal guidance is not about quickly selling a product as a solution. It is about understanding your everyday life:

- What are you really looking for?
- Is this about scent, care, massage, room, food routines or family?
- Which products do you already have?
- What does the product label allow?
- Are children, pets, pregnancy, medication or sensitive skin involved?
- Is a finished doTERRA product more appropriate than a DIY recipe?

This keeps the recommendation closer to what doTERRA actually offers, but without risky online promises.

## Safety

On Guard contains intense oil profiles. Around children, pets, topical use, internal use and DIY capsules, extra caution is needed. Do not publish illness or defensive capsule recipes on the website.

## In short

doTERRA On Guard can be positioned more clearly on Kyle Oils. Not as a remedy, but as a product area for family everyday life & safe use, safe routines and personal orientation.
