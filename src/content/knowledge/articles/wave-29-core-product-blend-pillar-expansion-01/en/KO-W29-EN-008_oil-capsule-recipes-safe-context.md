# Oil capsule recipes: a safety-first guide

Capsule recipes and internal use belongs to the product areas where people have very concrete expectations. That is exactly why public language needs the right balance: close enough to real everyday use, but without medical or exaggerated claims.

## Core area

Product choice, safety & personal guidance

## What doTERRA officially points toward

There are doTERRA products and recipe ideas involving internal use. Publicly on Kyle Oils, however, no DIY capsule recipes for illness, colds, flu or symptoms are published.

Kyle Oils uses this area through a clean guidance logic: What is the product? Which everyday situation is it searched for? Which safe-use context matters? Where does personal guidance begin?

## Safe public positioning

This page may place Capsule recipes and internal use like this:

- as a clear boundary between public website and personal guidance
- as an explanation of why products, labels and market matter more than internet recipes
- as protection against disease, prevention and treatment claims
- as a trust signal because Kyle does not publish every recipe publicly

## What we do not write publicly

These statements do not belong on the website:

- FluBomb recipe against illness
- when you get sick, fight it off immediately
- helps colds or flu
- Oregano/On Guard capsules as treatment

## How Kyle places this in guidance

Personal guidance is not about quickly selling a product as a solution. It is about understanding your everyday life:

- What are you really looking for?
- Is this about scent, care, massage, room, food routines or family?
- Which products do you already have?
- What does the product label allow?
- Are children, pets, pregnancy, medication or sensitive skin involved?
- Is a finished doTERRA product more appropriate than a DIY recipe?

This keeps the recommendation closer to what doTERRA actually offers, but without risky online promises.

## Safety

Internal use is not an area for public DIY simplification. It requires product labels, country/market, age group, medication, pregnancy, health context and clear doTERRA guidance. That is why it belongs in personal guidance, not as an illness recipe on a website.

## In short

Capsule recipes and internal use can be positioned more clearly on Kyle Oils. Not as a remedy, but as a product area for product choice, safety & personal guidance, safe routines and personal orientation.

## Personal guidance

If you want to know whether Capsule recipes and internal use fits you, ask Kyle. Together, you can calmly place product form, use, safety and everyday context.
