# Serenity: placing evening & sleep routine safely

doTERRA Serenity belongs to the product areas where people have very concrete expectations. That is exactly why public language needs the right balance: close enough to real everyday use, but without medical or exaggerated claims.

## Core area

Evening & sleep routine

## What doTERRA officially points toward

doTERRA positions Serenity around calm, relaxation, bedtime and the Essential Aromatics wellness categories. Kyle Oils can use this direction, but not as treatment for sleep problems.

Kyle Oils uses this area through a clean guidance logic: What is the product? Which everyday situation is it searched for? Which safe-use context matters? Where does personal guidance begin?

## Safe public positioning

This page may place doTERRA Serenity like this:

- for a calm evening environment
- as a scent anchor around bedtime and evening routine
- for people looking for a clear transition from day into evening
- as a guidance area for scent, amount, room and personal preference

## What we do not write publicly

These statements do not belong on the website:

- helps sleep disorders
- guarantees better sleep
- treats trouble falling asleep
- replaces sleep guidance or medical help

## How Kyle places this in guidance

Personal guidance is not about quickly selling a product as a solution. It is about understanding your everyday life:

- What are you really looking for?
- Is this about scent, care, massage, room, food routines or family?
- Which products do you already have?
- What does the product label allow?
- Are children, pets, pregnancy, medication or sensitive skin involved?
- Is a finished doTERRA product more appropriate than a DIY recipe?

This keeps the recommendation closer to what doTERRA actually offers, but without risky online promises.

## Safety

Evening scent should be sparing, pleasant and well ventilated. Bedrooms, children, pets and scent-sensitive people require extra restraint.

## In short

doTERRA Serenity can be positioned more clearly on Kyle Oils. Not as a remedy, but as a product area for evening & sleep routine, safe routines and personal orientation.
