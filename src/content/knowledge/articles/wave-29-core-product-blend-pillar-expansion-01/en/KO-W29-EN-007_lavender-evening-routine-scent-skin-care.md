# Lavender: evening routine, scent & skin care

doTERRA Lavender belongs to the product areas where people have very concrete expectations. That is exactly why public language needs the right balance: close enough to real everyday use, but without medical or exaggerated claims.

## Core area

Evening & sleep routine plus skin care & care feel

## What doTERRA officially points toward

doTERRA describes Lavender with its distinctive aroma, calming scent profile and cosmetic skin-care context. Kyle Oils can therefore place Lavender more clearly around evening routine and care feel.

Kyle Oils uses this area through a clean guidance logic: What is the product? Which everyday situation is it searched for? Which safe-use context matters? Where does personal guidance begin?

## Safe public positioning

This page may place doTERRA Lavender like this:

- for a calm evening environment and scent anchors around bedtime
- for people who want to start with a classic single oil
- for skin care, care feel and cosmetic context according to the label
- as a bridge between Oil Library, Routines and guidance

## What we do not write publicly

These statements do not belong on the website:

- heals wounds
- helps cuts
- treats sunburn
- treats sleep disorders

## How Kyle places this in guidance

Personal guidance is not about quickly selling a product as a solution. It is about understanding your everyday life:

- What are you really looking for?
- Is this about scent, care, massage, room, food routines or family?
- Which products do you already have?
- What does the product label allow?
- Are children, pets, pregnancy, medication or sensitive skin involved?
- Is a finished doTERRA product more appropriate than a DIY recipe?

This keeps the recommendation closer to what doTERRA actually offers, but without risky online promises.

## Safety

Lavender may feel soft aromatically, but it is still an essential oil. For topical use, follow the label, dilute, do not apply to open wounds and stop if a reaction occurs.
