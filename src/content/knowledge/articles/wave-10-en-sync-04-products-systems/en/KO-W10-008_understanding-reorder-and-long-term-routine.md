# Understanding reorder and long-term routine

The first order is only the beginning.

doTERRA products become more useful when you understand what you use regularly, what fits your life and what you do not need to reorder.

Reordering should be intentional, not automatic.

## Why reorder planning matters

After the first order, many people lose track.

Questions appear:

- What is almost empty?
- What did I actually use?
- What stayed untouched?
- What do I want again?
- What was just curiosity?
- Which routine worked?
- Which product confused me?
- What should I ask before buying more?

These questions make the second step better than the first.

## Do not reorder everything automatically

Just because a product is empty does not mean it must be replaced.

Ask:

- Did I use it regularly?
- Did it fit my routine?
- Would I miss it?
- Is there a better product for my need?
- Did I use it safely?
- Was it useful or just interesting?
- Do I still understand why I bought it?

A conscious reorder prevents unnecessary buying.

## What long-term routine means

A long-term routine does not mean using many products every day.

It may mean:

- one morning diffuser routine
- one evening routine
- one self-care product
- a clear storage place
- one reorder check every few weeks or months
- a simple list of what you actually use

Small repeated habits often matter more than large product plans.

## Inventory versus overwhelm

A small inventory can be practical. Too many products can become confusing.

A useful inventory comes from experience:

- what you use often
- what you use occasionally
- what is seasonal
- what needs safety attention
- what should not be bought again
- what is for later

Clarity matters more than quantity.

## After a starter kit

The first reorder after a starter kit is an important moment.

A good process:

1. review the products
2. mark what you used
3. identify what you liked
4. note what confused you
5. check safety questions
6. decide what to reorder
7. skip what did not fit
8. plan the next routine

This makes reordering more intentional.

## Customer account and overview

A customer account can help organize access and ordering.

But it should not become an automatic buying habit.

Use the account as a tool, not as pressure.

Kyle can help you review what makes sense before you reorder.

## Common mistakes

Avoid:

- reordering everything that is empty
- buying new products before understanding old ones
- not keeping notes
- forgetting what you already have
- starting too many systems at once
- focusing only on savings
- ordering because of excitement instead of need
- not asking questions after the first purchase

## A simple reorder routine

Every few weeks or months, ask:

- What do I use regularly?
- What is almost empty?
- What did not fit?
- What do I want to learn next?
- Which safety questions remain?
- What is truly needed for the next order?

This makes your doTERRA routine calmer and more sustainable.

## Summary

Reordering is not only a buying moment. It is a reflection moment.

It helps you understand which products truly belong in your life and which ones can wait.
