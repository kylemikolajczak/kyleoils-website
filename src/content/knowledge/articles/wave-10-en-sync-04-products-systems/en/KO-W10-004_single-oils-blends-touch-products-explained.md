# Single oils, blends and Touch products explained

When you first look at doTERRA products, you may see several terms: single oil, blend, Touch, roll-on, carrier oil and more.

This can feel confusing at first.

It becomes easier when you understand the different product forms.

## Single oils

A single oil is an essential oil from one plant source.

Examples include:

- Lavender
- Lemon
- Peppermint
- Frankincense
- Tea Tree
- Oregano
- Copaiba

Single oils are useful for learning because you can understand one scent profile at a time.

## Why single oils help beginners

Single oils help you ask:

- What does this oil smell like?
- Is it fresh, soft, woody, herbal or intense?
- Do I like it?
- Is it better for diffuser use or topical routines?
- Does it need special safety attention?
- Would I use it again?

This builds real understanding.

## Blends

A blend combines several essential oils into one product.

Blends can be helpful if you want a ready-made scent profile or a product connected to a specific routine.

A blend is not automatically gentler or safer than a single oil. It still needs label reading and safe use.

## Why blends can be useful

Blends can help if you:

- do not want to mix oils yourself
- want a ready-made scent experience
- prefer a product connected to a routine
- want fewer decisions
- like a balanced scent profile

They are different from single oils, not necessarily better.

## Touch products

Touch products are usually pre-diluted roll-on products.

They can be practical because they are easier to apply and often more beginner-friendly for topical routines.

Still, pre-diluted does not mean careless.

You should still:

- read the label
- avoid eyes and sensitive areas
- use appropriately
- consider children and sensitive skin
- stop if skin feels uncomfortable

## Which product form fits?

A single oil may fit when you want to learn one scent clearly.

A blend may fit when you want a finished scent profile or routine-oriented product.

A Touch product may fit when you want a simple topical format that is already diluted.

The right format depends on your routine, experience and comfort level.

## Common mistakes

Avoid:

- thinking blends are automatically safer
- using Touch products without reading labels
- applying single oils undiluted
- trying too many products at once
- confusing scent preference with safe use
- treating all product forms the same
- ignoring strong oils inside blends
