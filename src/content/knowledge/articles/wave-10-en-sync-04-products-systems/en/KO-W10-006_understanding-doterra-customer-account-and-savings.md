# Understanding the doTERRA customer account and savings

If you are interested in doTERRA, you may hear about a customer account and possible purchasing advantages.

This can be confusing at first.

The goal of this article is not to push you into creating an account. The goal is to help you understand what role an account may play.

## Why have a customer account?

A customer account can be useful if you want to order doTERRA products regularly.

Depending on the market, account type and current doTERRA conditions, certain purchasing advantages may be available.

These details can change, so current information should always be checked.

## Savings are not the main point

A purchasing advantage can be helpful, but it should not be the only reason to start.

More important questions are:

- Which products actually fit?
- Do you understand how to use them?
- Have you learned the safety basics?
- Will you use the products regularly?
- Do you want personal support?
- Is a starter kit useful or too much?
- What questions do you still have?

A discount is useful. Understanding is more important.

## A customer account does not mean sales pressure

Having a customer account does not automatically mean you need to sell products.

Many people start simply as customers because they want to use products for themselves and their family.

Kyle Oils separates these clearly:

- being a customer
- understanding products
- building routines
- getting support
- later exploring the business opportunity only if genuinely interested

There is no need to rush that decision.

## How Kyle helps

Kyle can help you sort the practical questions:

- Which account type is relevant?
- Which products make sense?
- What is optional?
- Which starter kit, if any, fits?
- What should your first order include?
- What safety questions should be answered?
- What support do you get after ordering?

This makes the process feel less technical and less sales-driven.

## Why support after ordering matters

Many people order products and then do not know what to do next.

After ordering, questions often appear:

- Which product should I use first?
- How many drops?
- Diffuser or topical use?
- Do I need dilution?
- What about children?
- What about sun exposure?
- How do I build a routine?
- When should I ask before using something?

A customer account is the technical access. Guidance makes the difference.

## What to clarify before starting

Before creating an account or choosing a larger order, ask:

- Am I only gathering information, or do I want to start?
- How big should my first step be?
- Do I understand the products?
- Do I want a starter kit?
- Is this for me, family, home or routines?
- Are there health questions?
- Do I want ongoing support?

These questions help prevent poor choices.

## Common misunderstandings

People sometimes think:

- I have to sell.
- I have to buy a large kit.
- I need to understand everything immediately.
- The discount matters more than safety.
- I am alone after ordering.
- A customer account creates pressure.

Good guidance should remove pressure, not create it.

## Summary

A doTERRA customer account can be useful if you want to use products regularly and benefit from current purchasing options.

But the real value is understanding what you order and how to use it safely.
