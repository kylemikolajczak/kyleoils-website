# Your first 7 days with essential oils

Your first week with essential oils should not feel overwhelming.

You do not need to understand every product, every blend or every routine immediately. A better first week is simple, safe and calm.

This 7-day path helps you explore essential oils step by step.

## Day 1: Understand what essential oils are

Start with the basics.

Essential oils are concentrated aromatic extracts from plants. They are not ordinary perfumes and should not be used casually.

Read about:

- what essential oils are
- why they are concentrated
- why a few drops are enough
- why safety matters
- why not all oils are the same

Do not try everything on day one.

## Day 2: Smell one oil consciously

Choose one oil and simply smell it.

Ask:

- Is it fresh, soft, warm, herbal or intense?
- Do I like it?
- Does it feel too strong?
- Would I want it in a room?
- Would I use it in the morning or evening?

This helps you build your own relationship with the oil instead of copying someone else’s opinion.

## Day 3: Learn diffuser basics

Before mixing many oils, learn how your diffuser works.

Check:

- how much water it uses
- how long it runs
- how to clean it
- where it should stand
- how many drops feel comfortable
- whether pets or children are present

Use less rather than more.

## Day 4: Try a short diffuser routine

Choose one scent and run the diffuser for a short time.

Notice the room:

- Is the scent pleasant?
- Is it too strong?
- Does the room need ventilation?
- Would you use this again?
- Does it fit morning, work or evening?

Do not run the diffuser all day as a beginner.

## Day 5: Learn dilution

Before using oils on your skin, understand dilution.

Learn:

- what a carrier oil is
- why dilution matters
- which oils are stronger
- how skin sensitivity works
- why children and sensitive skin need more caution

Do not apply oils undiluted just because someone online said it was fine.

## Day 6: Choose one simple routine

Now choose one small routine.

Examples:

- morning diffuser
- evening diffuser
- a simple self-care scent
- a tidy-home scent moment
- a short work-focus routine
- a learning routine with one oil

Keep it small enough to repeat.

## Day 7: Reflect and ask questions

After a week, ask:

- Which scent did I like?
- What felt too strong?
- Did I use too much?
- What safety question do I still have?
- Would I prefer diffuser, topical use or simple product guidance?
- Do I want to explore a starter set?
- Should I ask Kyle before choosing more products?

Reflection prevents random buying.

## What not to do in the first week

Avoid:

- using too many oils at once
- applying strong oils directly to the skin
- ignoring labels
- using oils internally without proper product guidance
- diffusing all day
- copying extreme routines from social media
- buying many products before understanding the basics

## A good first week feels calm

You should end the first week with more clarity, not more confusion.

A good result is:

- I know what essential oils are.
- I understand that they are concentrated.
- I know why dilution matters.
- I used a diffuser carefully.
- I found one scent I like.
- I know what I want to ask next.

That is enough.

## Summary

Your first 7 days with essential oils should be simple, safe and slow.

Start with understanding, then scent, then diffuser basics, then dilution and only then build a small routine.
