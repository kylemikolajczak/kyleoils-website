# Blue Tansy: blue scent note for body care and massage context

Blue Tansy is a distinctive blue oil often placed through scent, body care, skin feel and massage context.

## Focus

blue oil, body care, massage context, scent blends

## How to place Blue Tansy

Blue Tansy is a distinctive oil with striking color and a soft, sweet-herbal scent profile. Many people know it indirectly because it is part of doTERRA Deep Blue.

## Scent profile

The scent is soft, herbal, slightly sweet and very individual. Blue Tansy is not an oil to put casually into every blend.

## Typical Kyle Oils placement

Kyle would place Blue Tansy more in body care, massage context, after active days, scent blends and skin feel. Not as a pain or injury promise.

## Safe use

Blue Tansy can stain. Dose carefully, be mindful of textiles, dilute for topical use and read the label. Test slowly with sensitive skin.

## Fits better if

you find Deep Blue interesting, like special scent profiles or want to understand an oil for conscious body-care and massage contexts.

## Wait if

you are still very new, prefer bright and simple scents or do not need intense single oils.

## In short

Blue Tansy is a specialty oil. It is interesting, but not a must-have oil for every starter.
