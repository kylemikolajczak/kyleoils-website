# Arborvitae: woody scent, calm and conscious use

Arborvitae is a deep woody scent that fits room scent, wood-care context and calm diffuser blends.

## Focus

woody scent, room scent, conscious diffuser blends

## How to place Arborvitae

Arborvitae is a very woody oil. It is not a typical beginner oil for everyone, but it is an interesting building block for people who like deep, clear wood notes.

## Scent profile

The scent is warm, woody, dry and distinct. Even a small amount can have a strong presence in a blend.

## Typical Kyle Oils placement

Kyle would place Arborvitae more in room scent, calm diffuser blends, wood-care context or as a counterpoint to very sweet and floral oils.

## Safe use

Start with a very small amount. For topical use, dilute and follow the label. Do not use casually around children, pets or sensitive skin.

## Fits better if

you like forest, wood and sauna-like scent worlds or want to build natural, deep diffuser blends.

## Wait if

you are just starting with oils and are not yet sure whether intense wood notes fit you.

## In short

Arborvitae is a characterful woody oil. Placed well, it feels elegant, calm and very conscious.
