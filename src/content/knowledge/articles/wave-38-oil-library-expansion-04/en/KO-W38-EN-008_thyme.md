# Thyme: herbal oil between kitchen, scent and caution

Thyme is a strong herbal oil that should be placed very consciously between kitchen, scent and topical use.

## Focus

herbal oil, kitchen, dilution, strong use

## How to place Thyme

Thyme is familiar to many people as a kitchen herb. As an essential oil, it is far more concentrated and needs calmer placement.

## Scent profile

The scent is herbal, warm, spicy and present. Thyme can quickly dominate if used too generously.

## Typical Kyle Oils placement

Kyle would place Thyme as a strong herbal oil: interesting for kitchen use according to label, spicy scent blends and conscious use. Not as a health promise.

## Safe use

Thyme is not an oil for large amounts. Topical use only diluted, carefully and according to label. Internal use only when the product label and market clearly allow it.

## Fits better if

you understand herbal oils, can separate kitchen and oil use consciously and do not want to copy internet recipes.

## Wait if

you are still a beginner, have sensitive skin or have not yet received clear guidance about strong oils.

## In short

Thyme is powerful in scent and demanding in placement. Less is usually wiser here.
