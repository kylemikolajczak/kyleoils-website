# Petitgrain: green, fresh and calmly placed

Petitgrain is a green, fresh oil from the bitter orange tree that can be placed in scent, evening and body-care routines.

## Focus

green-fresh scent, room scent, evening routine, body care

## How to place Petitgrain

In the oil world, Petitgrain feels like a calm space between citrus, green and soft woody notes. It comes from the bitter orange tree and is distilled from leaves and twigs.

## Scent profile

The scent is fresh, green, slightly floral and less bright than typical citrus oils. This makes Petitgrain useful when Orange or Lemon feel too sparkling.

## Typical Kyle Oils placement

Kyle would place Petitgrain more as a calm scent anchor: evening routine, diffuser, bath, body care or a moment where the room should feel clearer and softer.

## Safe use

Start aromatically and with little scent. For topical use: dilute, patch test, read the label and stay careful with sensitive skin.

## Fits better if

you like green, fresh and not-too-sweet scents, want a soft alternative to classic citrus oils or want your evening routine to be less lavender-heavy.

## Wait if

you do not yet know which scent families you like or are already experimenting with too many oils.

## In short

Petitgrain is not a loud oil. It is a calm, green scent building block for people who like fine scent profiles.
