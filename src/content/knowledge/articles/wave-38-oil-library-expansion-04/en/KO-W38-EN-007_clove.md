# Clove: warm spice oil with a clear dilution boundary

Clove is an intense warm spice oil that should be placed especially carefully because of its strength.

## Focus

spice oil, hot oil, dilution, seasonal scent

## How to place Clove

Clove is an intense warm spice oil. Many people know it through seasonal scents or as part of doTERRA On Guard.

## Scent profile

The scent is warm, spicy, heavy and very present. One drop can change a blend a lot.

## Typical Kyle Oils placement

Kyle would not place Clove as a casual beginner oil, but as a strong oil for clear situations: seasonal diffuser blends, warm room-scent ideas or product understanding around On Guard.

## Safe use

Clove is a strong oil. For topical use, only heavily diluted, according to label and with care. Not in children’s hands, not near eyes or mucous membranes and not as a spontaneous DIY experiment.

## Fits better if

you like spice scents, want to understand seasonal blends and are ready to take dilution seriously.

## Wait if

you are just starting, have sensitive skin or tend to use strong oils too generously.

## In short

Clove can feel warm and valuable. But it needs respect, amount control and clear boundaries.
