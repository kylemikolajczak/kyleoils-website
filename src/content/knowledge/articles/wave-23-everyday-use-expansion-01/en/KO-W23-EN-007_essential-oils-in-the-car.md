# Can you use essential oils in the car?

The car can feel like a practical place for scent. At the same time, it is a small enclosed space, heats up quickly and is a place where attention matters.

## Transport and storage

Do not leave oils in a hot car. Heat and direct light can negatively affect essential oils. If you transport oils, take them back inside.

## Scent while driving

If you use scent in the car, keep it very subtle and non-distracting. Intense mint, herbal, floral or spice notes can quickly become too much in a small interior.

## A better use idea

Scent the car very lightly before driving or use a passive solution that does not drip, leak or damage surfaces. Open a window if needed.

## What to avoid

Avoid bottles in the cup holder in sunlight, drops on dashboard, leather, plastic or fabric, experiments while driving and strong scent blends in a closed car.

## In short

In the car: do not store, do not overdo, do not distract.
