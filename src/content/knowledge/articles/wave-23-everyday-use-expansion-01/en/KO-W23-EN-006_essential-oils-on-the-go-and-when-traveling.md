# Essential oils on the go and when traveling

When you are on the go, you do not need your entire oil collection. A good travel or on-the-go routine is small, well packed and clearly chosen.

## Take less

Choose only products you truly use: maybe one favorite scent, one Touch product or one small roll-on. Everything else is usually better left at home.

## Packaging matters

Oils should be tightly closed, upright or securely stored and protected from heat, light and leaking. Bags, suitcases and cars can become warm quickly.

## Hotel rooms or unfamiliar spaces

Use extra restraint in unfamiliar rooms. Not every space is well ventilated, and not every material tolerates sprays or oil residue. Ventilate first, then scent very lightly.

## What to avoid

Avoid open bottles in luggage, drops on furniture or bedding, intense diffuser sessions in small hotel rooms and leaving oils in a hot car.

## In short

On the go, the best oil routine is small, well packed and consciously chosen.
