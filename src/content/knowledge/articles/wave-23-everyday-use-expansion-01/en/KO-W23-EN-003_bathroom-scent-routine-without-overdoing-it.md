# Bathroom scent routine without overdoing it

The bathroom is an obvious place for fresh scents. At the same time, it is often small, warm and not very ventilated. This means: less, shorter and more intentional.

## Why bathrooms become intense quickly

Small rooms hold scent more strongly. Heat and humidity can make a scent feel more present. What feels pleasant in a living room can quickly become too much in a bathroom.

## Which scent profiles fit?

Fresh citrus notes, green herbal profiles or clear conifer and wood notes can work well. Heavy floral or spice notes should be tested sparingly.

## A simple bathroom routine

Ventilate briefly, clean first, use only a few drops or a very short diffuser time and observe whether the room stays pleasant. The scent should feel fresh, not dominate the air.

## Important safety

Do not drop oils directly onto bathtubs, showers, tile grout or sensitive surfaces. Be careful with slippery residue, eyes, mucous membranes and children.

## In short

In the bathroom, scent becomes stronger faster. A good bathroom routine is fresh, short and not overloaded.
