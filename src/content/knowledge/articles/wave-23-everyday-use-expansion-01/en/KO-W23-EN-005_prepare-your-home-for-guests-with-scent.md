# How to prepare your home for guests with scent

When guests visit, a home should feel pleasant. Scent can contribute to that, but only when it stays subtle. A guest should feel welcomed, not hit by a wall of fragrance.

## Subtle feels more premium

A good guest scent stays in the background. It supports the atmosphere without demanding attention. If someone comments strongly on the scent immediately, it may already be too much.

## Prepare instead of constantly diffusing

You can scent the room briefly beforehand, then ventilate or turn the diffuser off before guests arrive. This leaves a light scent signature without overloading the air.

## Consider sensitive guests

Some people are sensitive to scent. Children, pregnant guests, pets or people with respiratory or fragrance sensitivity should be considered. When unsure, use less or skip the diffuser.

## Which scent profiles fit?

Bright citrus notes, soft wood notes or light floral-herbal profiles can feel friendly. Very heavy, sweet, minty or spicy scents can be riskier for guests.

## In short

For guests: better pleasantly barely noticeable than impressively strong.
