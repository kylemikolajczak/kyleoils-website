# How to create a home office scent zone

In a home office, scent can help mark a space more consciously: start of work, break, finish. This is not a concentration promise. It is a simple space and routine logic.

## Why scent zones can help

When living and working happen in the same space, clear transitions are often missing. A recurring, light scent can be a signal: a certain part of the day begins now.

## Which scent profiles fit?

Clear, fresh profiles often fit a work area: Lemon, Grapefruit, Rosemary, Spearmint, Cypress or Cedarwood. The more intense an oil is, the more sparingly it should be used.

## A simple 3-zone idea

Use a bright scent to start, a neutral break without scent and a softer scent to finish. This keeps scent as a conscious signal instead of constant background.

## What to avoid

Avoid strong blends during video calls, continuous diffusion for many hours, intense mint or herbal oils without breaks and continued use if you notice pressure in the head or discomfort.

## In short

A home office scent zone should structure the room, not promise performance.
