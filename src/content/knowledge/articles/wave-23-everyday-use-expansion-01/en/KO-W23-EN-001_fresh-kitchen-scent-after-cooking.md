# Fresh kitchen scent after cooking

After cooking, a kitchen can smell like spices, oil, onions, coffee or warm food. Essential oils can help reset the room scent. The key is not to cover everything up, but to ventilate, clean and then scent lightly.

## Ventilate first, then scent

A diffuser does not replace fresh air. Open a window first, remove food leftovers and clean the most important surfaces. Then a fresh scent can feel much clearer because it is not fighting old smells.

## Which scent profiles fit?

Citrus notes like Lemon, Lime, Grapefruit or Wild Orange often feel bright and clean. Herbal profiles like Rosemary or Cilantro can be interesting in a kitchen, but should be used sparingly. Wood notes can make the scent feel rounder.

## A simple routine

Choose two oils or one ready-made scent profile, start with a few drops and do not run the diffuser longer than needed. Check whether children, pets or scent-sensitive people are present.

## What to avoid

Do not drop oils directly onto kitchen surfaces, cutting boards or food. Do not use scent as a replacement for cleaning. Do not diffuse intensely for long periods in a small room.

## In short

A fresh kitchen comes from sequence: ventilate, clean, scent lightly. This makes the scent feel clearer and more premium.
