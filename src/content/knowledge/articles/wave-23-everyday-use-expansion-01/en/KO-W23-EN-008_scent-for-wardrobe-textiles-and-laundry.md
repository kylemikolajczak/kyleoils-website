# Scent for wardrobe, textiles and laundry?

Essential oils are often connected with fresh laundry, wardrobe scent or fabric sprays. That can be pleasant, but it needs more care than many DIY tips suggest.

## Why fabrics are sensitive

Fabrics can hold stains, residue or strong scent. Some materials are sensitive. Essential oils should not simply be dropped directly onto clothing, bedding, towels or upholstery.

## Think indirectly

If you want wardrobe scent, think more indirectly: very subtle, not directly on valuable fabrics, not on children’s textiles and not where pets can easily access it.

## Laundry and heat

Be careful with oil ideas around washing machines, dryers, irons or heat. Do not use spontaneous recipes if you cannot judge the material, appliance or safety context.

## What is easier

For beginners, a subtle room scent in the bedroom or dressing area is often easier to understand than direct fabric use. If you want to scent textiles, test first on an inconspicuous area.

## In short

Textiles hold scent. So the rule is: indirect, sparing, material-aware and no experimenting with valuable fabrics.
