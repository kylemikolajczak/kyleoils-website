# Do I have to buy a large starter kit?

Why a large kit is not required and how to place the start in a way that fits.

## The question

Do I have to buy a large starter kit right away?

## The short answer

No. A large kit can make sense, but it is not required.

## When a larger kit may fit

A larger kit can fit when you truly want to cover several everyday areas, are ready to learn and have good guidance.

## When smaller is better

A smaller start can be better when you first want to smell, test, ask questions or start with only a few products.

## What Kyle clarifies with you

Kyle looks with you at everyday life, budget, safety, children, pets, scent preferences and your actual motivation. That makes it much easier to see which kit makes sense.

## In short

The right starter kit is not automatically the largest one. It is the kit you understand and use.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure about this question, that is exactly a good reason for a short conversation. You do not need to prepare anything and you do not have to decide immediately. Kyle helps you calmly place what makes sense for you.
