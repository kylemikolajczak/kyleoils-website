# Why not Amazon or cheaper oils?

Why comparing doTERRA, cheaper oils and Amazon is not only a price question.

## The question

Why should I not simply buy cheaper oils on Amazon?

## The honest answer

You can find cheaper oils online. The point is that you are often alone with quality, use, context and safety.

## What to look at

With essential oils, price and scent are not the only factors. Origin, product information, label, use context, tolerance and whether you truly understand what you are buying all matter.

## Why doTERRA is perceived differently

doTERRA as a brand is strongly built around product quality, standards, community and guidance. For many people, that is the reason not to simply look for the cheapest oil.

## What Kyle adds

Kyle helps you compare products not only by price, but by how much sense they make for your everyday life.

## In short

Cheaper is not automatically wrong. But cheap and alone is not the same as conscious, guided and understandable.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure about this question, that is exactly a good reason for a short conversation. You do not need to prepare anything and you do not have to decide immediately. Kyle helps you calmly place what makes sense for you.
