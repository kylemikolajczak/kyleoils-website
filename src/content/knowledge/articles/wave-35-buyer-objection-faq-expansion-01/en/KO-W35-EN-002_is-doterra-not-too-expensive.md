# Is doTERRA not too expensive?

How to place the price of doTERRA more calmly, without pressure and without purchase promises.

## The question

Is doTERRA not simply too expensive?

## The honest answer

doTERRA is not the cheapest option on the market. There is no need to pretend otherwise.

## The better question

The better question is not only: What does one bottle cost? It is also: What will you actually use it for, how long will it last, does it fit your everyday life and do you understand safe use?

## Why guidance matters

An expensive product you do not use is not a good decision. A product you understand, use meaningfully and place safely can feel very different.

## What Kyle does not do

Kyle will not convince you that you need the largest kit. If a smaller start fits better, that is the more honest recommendation.

## In short

Yes, doTERRA is positioned as a high-quality option and it is not cheap. That is why the start should be conscious, not impulsive.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure about this question, that is exactly a good reason for a short conversation. You do not need to prepare anything and you do not have to decide immediately. Kyle helps you calmly place what makes sense for you.

## Note

Prices, kits, programs, account types and terms can change. For binding details, the current official doTERRA information in the relevant market always applies.
