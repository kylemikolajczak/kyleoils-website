# Can I become an advisor later?

Why you do not have to decide immediately and how the advisor idea can be placed without pressure.

## The question

Do I have to know immediately whether I want to become an advisor later?

## The short answer

No. You can first be a customer, try, learn and see whether doTERRA fits you at all.

## Why the idea can come later

Some people start only with products. Later they notice that they enjoy talking about them, sharing experiences or guiding other people.

## What is not promised

Kyle Oils does not promise income, rank or fast success. A doTERRA activity requires learning, relationships, consistency and clean communication.

## How Kyle places it

If you become curious at some point, Kyle calmly explains what a Wellness Advocate means and how team onboarding can support you. Without an immediate decision.

## In short

Becoming an advisor is a possibility, not a must. First comes real interest, then understanding, then decision.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure about this question, that is exactly a good reason for a short conversation. You do not need to prepare anything and you do not have to decide immediately. Kyle helps you calmly place what makes sense for you.
