# Do I have to order monthly?

LRP, reordering and monthly routines explained without pressure.

## The question

Do I have to order every month when I start with doTERRA?

## The short answer

No, starting with doTERRA does not automatically mean you have to order monthly.

## Where the question comes from

doTERRA has the Loyalty Rewards Program, or LRP, for recurring orders. That can make sense when you use products regularly.

## Why LRP does not fit everyone immediately

If you first want to get to know doTERRA, LRP does not have to be your first step. It should first be clear which products you actually use and whether a recurring structure makes sense at all.

## What Kyle clarifies with you

Kyle calmly explains what one-time order, customer account and LRP mean. Without pressure into a monthly routine.

## In short

Ordering monthly is not required for your first contact. It can become useful later when you truly work with doTERRA regularly.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure about this question, that is exactly a good reason for a short conversation. You do not need to prepare anything and you do not have to decide immediately. Kyle helps you calmly place what makes sense for you.

## Note

Prices, kits, programs, account types and terms can change. For binding details, the current official doTERRA information in the relevant market always applies.
