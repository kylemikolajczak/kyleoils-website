# Why through Kyle instead of directly in the doTERRA shop?

Why personal orientation before buying is often more useful than clicking through the shop alone.

## The question

Why should I go through Kyle if I can also look directly in the shop?

## The short answer

You can of course look directly. The difference is not the link, but the guidance around it.

## What can feel difficult in the shop

doTERRA has many oils, blends, kits, wellness products, account types and programs. When you are new, that can quickly feel big. You may buy something that looks good but does not really fit your everyday life.

## What Kyle adds

Kyle helps you clarify calmly before buying: What are you looking for? Which everyday situation is really behind it? Which kit may be too much? Which product makes sense? Where do you need safety context?

## Why this builds trust

You do not simply get a shop link. You get a person who listens, places things honestly and also says when a smaller kit or no purchase is more sensible right now.

## In short

Starting through Kyle does not mean more pressure. It means more orientation before the decision.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure about this question, that is exactly a good reason for a short conversation. You do not need to prepare anything and you do not have to decide immediately. Kyle helps you calmly place what makes sense for you.

## Note

Prices, kits, programs, account types and terms can change. For binding details, the current official doTERRA information in the relevant market always applies.
