# What happens after the purchase?

Why the purchase is not the end and how Kyle supports you after starting.

## The question

Am I alone after the purchase?

## The short answer

No. The purchase is not the end, but the beginning of your product experience.

## Why support matters

Many people buy oils and end up using only one or two products because they are missing a simple plan. This is where support makes a difference.

## What Kyle does after the purchase

Kyle helps you place your products, understand first routines, clarify safety questions and avoid trying to do everything at once.

## Team materials and onboarding

If you go deeper, materials and onboarding processes from Kyle’s team may also be added. The public website does not replace that. It first connects you with Kyle.

## In short

You do not simply buy and then get left alone. The goal is that you truly understand what you use.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you are unsure about this question, that is exactly a good reason for a short conversation. You do not need to prepare anything and you do not have to decide immediately. Kyle helps you calmly place what makes sense for you.

Prices, kits, programs, account types and terms can change. For binding details, the current official doTERRA information in the relevant market always applies.
