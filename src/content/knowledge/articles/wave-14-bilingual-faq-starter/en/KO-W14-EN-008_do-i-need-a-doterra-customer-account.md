# Do I need a doTERRA customer account?

A doTERRA customer account can make sense if you want to use doTERRA products regularly.

But it is not the first step for everyone.

The better question is: do you already understand what you need and how to use it safely?

## The simple answer

You do not need a customer account just to gather information.

An account can make sense if:

- you want to order products
- you plan to reorder regularly
- you want to use available purchasing advantages
- you want support after ordering
- you want to understand a starter kit
- you want to use products long-term

## What a customer account does not mean

A customer account does not automatically mean you need to sell products.

It also does not mean you need to buy a large starter kit.

Kyle Oils clearly separates:

- being a customer
- understanding products
- building routines
- getting support
- exploring the business opportunity later only if genuinely interested

No pressure.

## Why guidance before account setup helps

Before ordering, it helps to understand:

- Which products fit?
- What is beginner-friendly?
- What is too much?
- Which use is safe?
- Do I need a starter kit?
- Would a smaller start be better?
- Are there questions around children, pets or health?

An account does not answer these questions automatically. Guidance helps.

## How to think about purchasing advantages

A possible purchasing advantage can be useful.

But a discount is not a good reason to buy something you do not understand or will not use.

Better:

orientation first, decision second.

## In short

A doTERRA customer account can make sense if you genuinely want to start or order regularly.

For information only, you do not need an account first.
