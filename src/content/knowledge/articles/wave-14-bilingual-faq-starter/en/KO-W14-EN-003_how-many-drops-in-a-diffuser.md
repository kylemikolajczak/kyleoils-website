# How many drops should I use in a diffuser?

There is no single perfect number of drops for every diffuser and every room.

A better question is: how little is enough for the scent to feel pleasant?

## The simple answer

For beginners:

Start low and observe.

A diffuser should create a pleasant room atmosphere, not overpower the space.

The right amount depends on:

- room size
- diffuser size
- oil intensity
- runtime
- ventilation
- children in the room
- pets in the room
- personal scent sensitivity

## Why less is often better

Essential oils are concentrated. A few drops can already be clearly noticeable.

If you start too strongly, the scent can quickly become unpleasant. Small rooms need extra care.

## Use strong oils carefully

Some oils can feel very intense in a diffuser.

Examples may include:

- Peppermint
- Oregano
- Cinnamon Bark
- Clove
- strong herbal oils

These should not be treated like soft everyday scents.

## Who is in the room?

Before turning on the diffuser, ask:

- Am I alone?
- Are children present?
- Are pets nearby?
- Is anyone scent-sensitive?
- Is the room well ventilated?
- Can someone leave the room if the scent bothers them?

Scent should not be forced onto anyone.

## Runtime matters too

The number of drops is only part of the question. Runtime matters as well.

A few drops for a short time may feel pleasant. The same amount for many hours may be too much.

## In short

With a diffuser:

Start with less, diffuse for a shorter time and adjust slowly.

If the scent feels pleasant, it is enough.
