# What should I consider around pets?

Extra care is important around pets.

Animals experience scent differently from humans. What smells pleasant to you may be too intense or uncomfortable for an animal.

## The simple answer

If pets live in your home, use essential oils very consciously.

Pay attention to:

- good ventilation
- short diffuser times
- low scent intensity
- an exit option for the pet
- observing behavior
- no direct application without professional guidance
- safe storage
- no experiments

## Diffusers and pets

If you use a diffuser, your pet should be able to leave the room.

Use:

- fewer drops
- shorter runtimes
- larger rooms
- open doors
- good ventilation

If your pet becomes restless, avoids the room, sneezes, drools, coughs or behaves differently, stop diffusing and ventilate the room.

## Do not replace veterinary care

Essential oils should not be used to treat pet symptoms or conditions.

If your pet has symptoms, illness or unusual behavior, speak with a veterinarian.

Kyle Oils does not replace veterinary guidance.

## Be especially careful with cats

Cats are generally considered especially sensitive to many substances and scents.

For cats, be even more cautious: ventilate, avoid direct application without professional guidance and avoid strong scent experiments.

## What to avoid

Avoid:

- running a diffuser continuously
- using strong oils in small closed rooms
- applying oils to fur or skin without professional guidance
- adding oils to pet food
- trying to treat pets with oils
- storing oils where pets can reach them
- ignoring animal behavior

## In short

Around pets: use less, ventilate well and observe carefully.

A pleasant room scent for humans is not automatically suitable for animals.
