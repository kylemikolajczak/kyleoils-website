# What if I do not like a scent?

You do not have to like every scent. That is not a mistake and does not mean essential oils are not for you. Scent is personal.

## Scent is individual

An oil can feel beautiful to one person and too heavy, too herbal, too sweet or too intense to another. That is normal. Your nose is an important part of your selection.

## Do not give up immediately

Some scents feel very strong straight from the bottle, but much softer in a diffuser or small blend. Still, you do not need to force yourself to use something you dislike.

## What you can test

Try fewer drops, shorter diffusion time, combining it with citrus, wood or floral notes, or choosing a different scent family. Sometimes the oil is not the problem; the use method is.

## What not to do

Do not blindly buy more products to rescue a scent. Do not continue using anything if it makes you feel nauseous, pressured in the head or uncomfortable.

## In short

If you do not like a scent, that is information. Not failure. Good guidance helps turn that into a better choice.
