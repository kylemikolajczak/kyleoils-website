# Which oils can be too strong for beginners?

Some essential oils are not wrong for beginners, but they are more intense. That means: more respect, less amount and better context.

## Which oils often feel more intense

For beginners, spice oils, very herbal oils, mint oils and some citrus oils can feel more intense. Depending on the product, examples include Oregano, Cinnamon, Clove, Peppermint, Rosemary, Eucalyptus, Lemongrass or Bergamot.

## Why strong is not better

A strong scent can be impressive, but it is not automatically more useful. Overly intense routines are often abandoned faster because they feel unpleasant, too present or hard to understand.

## What beginners should watch

Pay attention to scent intensity, skin tolerance, product label, dilution, photosensitivity, children, pets and room size. With strong oils especially: start slowly and do not experiment.

## A better beginner logic

Start with oils you can understand well and add intense oils later. A clear scent profile is better than a complicated arsenal.

## In short

Strong oils are not bad. They simply do not always belong at the beginning and need clear boundaries.
