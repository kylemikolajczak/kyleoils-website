# Can I blend essential oils?

Yes, essential oils can be blended. But good blends are not created by putting as many oils together as possible. Good blends are clear, pleasant and easy to understand.

## Start simple

Start with two or three oils. For example, one citrus note, one wood or herbal note and maybe one soft floral note. This helps you understand which oil contributes which part of the scent.

## Understand scent families

Citrus oils often feel bright and fresh. Wood oils add depth. Floral oils can feel soft or intense. Mint and spice oils can become very present. This logic is often more helpful than complicated recipes.

## Safety still matters

A blend is not automatically gentler. If it contains a strong oil, it is still a strong oil. For topical use, dilution, product labels, skin sensitivity and amount matter.

## What beginners should avoid

Avoid ten oils in one blend, strong oils without a dilution plan, long-running blends in small rooms and DIY recipes without understanding the oils included.

## In short

Blending makes sense when you start small, understand scent families and do not ignore safety.
