# Why is a small amount enough?

Essential oils are concentrated plant extracts. That is why the common beginner mistake is often not “too little,” but “too much.”

## Understand concentration

An essential oil is not a supermarket room fragrance and not a light cream. It is highly concentrated. One drop can already be noticeable in a diffuser, diluted topical routine or scent blend.

## More drops do not mean a better routine

More drops can make a scent too intense, place unnecessary stress on skin or mucous membranes or be too much around children, pets and scent-sensitive people. A good routine feels pleasant, repeatable and safe to understand.

## Less helps you learn

When you start with less, you can better notice how an oil smells, how long it stays in the room and whether it fits your everyday life. Too much scent can overwhelm your own perception.

## Practical starting point

Diffuse sparingly, dilute for topical use and explore new oils one at a time. Then slowly learn what feels pleasant for you.

## In short

Small amounts are not a weakness. They are the normal, sensible way to work with concentrated products.
