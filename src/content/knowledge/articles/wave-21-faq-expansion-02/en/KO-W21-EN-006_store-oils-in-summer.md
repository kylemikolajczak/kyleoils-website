# How should I store oils in summer?

Summer is not automatically a problem for essential oils. The main issues are strong heat, direct sunlight and storing oils in a car.

## Why storage matters

doTERRA advises keeping oils away from excessive heat and light because these can affect chemical properties. Even when a bottle is closed, it should not be exposed to extreme conditions unnecessarily.

## What to avoid

Do not leave oils in a hot car, on a windowsill, in direct sunlight or permanently in a very warm bathroom. In summer, these places can become too warm quickly.

## A better storage place

Store oils cool, dry, dark and out of reach of children. A drawer, box or cupboard is often better than displaying bottles on a windowsill.

## Summer on the go

If you take oils with you, use a small pouch or box and do not leave them in the car. Bring only what you really need instead of carrying the whole collection.

## In short

Summer storage is simple: cool, dark, dry, closed and not in a hot car.
