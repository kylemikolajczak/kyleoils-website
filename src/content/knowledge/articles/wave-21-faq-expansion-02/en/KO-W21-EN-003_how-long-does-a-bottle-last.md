# How long does a bottle last?

How long a bottle lasts depends strongly on how you use it. A bottle used daily in a diffuser will run out differently than an oil used only occasionally in a blend.

## A rough orientation

doTERRA mentions approximately 250 drops for several 15 mL bottles. This is not an exact formula for every oil and every situation, but it is a useful beginner orientation.

## Why it varies

Drop size, viscosity, bottle insert, oil type, temperature and use all influence how quickly a bottle empties. Your routine matters too: daily, weekly, occasional or used in several blends.

## A simple example, not a promise

If you use an average of 3 drops in a diffuser routine, around 250 drops can theoretically mean many individual uses. In practice, it depends on whether you diffuse daily, several times a day or only occasionally.

## How to plan better

Observe the first four weeks: Which bottle do you really use? Which one stays untouched? Which routine do you repeat? Then you can plan reorders much more intelligently.

## In short

A bottle does not last according to the calendar. It lasts according to your routine. Good planning saves more than spontaneous reordering.
