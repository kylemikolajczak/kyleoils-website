# What does CPTG mean?

CPTG stands for “Certified Pure Tested Grade” at doTERRA. It is doTERRA’s quality and testing standard used to evaluate essential oils for purity, composition and possible adulteration.

## What CPTG means in practice

doTERRA describes CPTG as a multi-step testing process. Oils are checked for chemical composition, adulteration, residues and quality. The goal is that an oil matches its defined profile.

## What CPTG does not mean

CPTG is not a medical promise. It does not mean every use is appropriate, that reactions are impossible or that safety rules no longer matter. Quality and safe use are two different topics.

## Why it still matters

Purity matters with essential oils because small amounts are highly concentrated. When you buy an oil, you want to know that the product is not random, diluted or misrepresented.

## A better way to place it

CPTG can be a quality argument. Your use should still be decided through the product label, dilution, everyday context, children, pets, skin and personal questions.

## In short

CPTG helps you understand doTERRA’s quality standard. It does not replace safe, conscious use.
