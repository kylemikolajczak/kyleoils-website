# Patchouli

Patchouli is a single essential oil with a clear scent profile and its own role in the doTERRA range. On Kyle Oils, this oil is not explained as a quick solution for complaints, but as part of scent, care, everyday life, product choice and safe guidance.

## Botanical name

Pogostemon cablin

## Scent family

Earthy-woody oil

## Scent profile

earthy, woody, warm, deep, slightly sweet and long-lasting

## Core area

Skin care, grounding scent & base note

## What doTERRA officially points toward

doTERRA positions Patchouli partly in a skin-care context and mentions dilution to minimize skin sensitivity.

Kyle Oils translates this product context into language visitors can understand: What do I smell? Why are people looking for this oil? Where does it fit into routines? Which safety boundaries matter?

## Safe public positioning

This oil can be placed publicly like this:

- for skin care and care feel according to the label
- as a deep base note in blends
- for people who prefer earthy oils instead of bright citrus profiles
- as a bridge between Oil Library, skin care and scent families

## What we do not write

These statements do not belong on the public website:

- treat skin problems
- heal acne or eczema
- promise psychological grounding

## How to get to know this oil

Do not start with the question of what an oil supposedly can do. Start with four simpler questions:

- How does it smell to you?
- Is it more bright, warm, herbal, woody, spicy or deep?
- Does it fit room scent, care, kitchen logic, massage or evening routine?
- Which use is intended by the label?

This builds real oil competence without relying on exaggerated promises.

## Safety context

Patchouli is deep and long-lasting. Use topically only by label, dilute and test slowly on sensitive skin. The scent can quickly dominate.

## Guidance from Kyle

If you want to know whether Patchouli fits your situation, ask Kyle. Especially with strong, warm, skin-related or food-adjacent oils, personal context is more useful than a quick internet rule.
