# Using a diffuser safely in small rooms

Small rooms can make scent feel intense faster. What is pleasant in a living room can become too much in a bathroom, bedroom, hallway or home office.

## Why small rooms are different

In small rooms, scent has less space to disperse. Heat, humidity and closed doors can make it feel stronger. So it is not only about which oil you use, but how long, how much and how well the space is ventilated.

## A safer starting logic

Start with fewer drops, a short run time and an open window or good ventilation. Observe whether the scent stays pleasant. If you constantly notice it strongly, it was probably too much.

## Who should be considered?

Children, pets, pregnant people, visitors and scent-sensitive people should always be considered. Room scent is only good when it remains pleasant for the people in the room.

## What to avoid

Avoid continuous diffusion in closed small rooms, strong spice or mint profiles without breaks and diffusers directly beside beds, pet spaces or children’s areas.

## In short

In small rooms: fewer drops, shorter use, better ventilation and always observe.

## Personal guidance

If you are unsure whether a use fits your home, ask Kyle. Especially around children, pets, sensitive skin, pregnancy, medication or small rooms, calm guidance is more useful than spontaneous experimenting.
