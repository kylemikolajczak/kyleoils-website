# Storing and transporting essential oils safely

Safe use does not start only when you use a drop. It starts with storage, transport and the question of who can access the bottles.

## Out of reach

Oils should be stored out of reach and sight of children and pets. Even small bottles can look interesting to children.

## Avoid heat and light

Strong heat, direct sun and storing oils in the car are not ideal. Oils should stay cool, dry, dark and tightly closed.

## Plan transport safely

For bags, travel or car transport, closed cases are better than loose bottles. Check that lids are tight and bottles are upright or protected.

## Do not place them anywhere

Do not store oils on windowsills, heaters, in the bathroom near heat sources or within reach of children. Visitors or babysitters should also know these are not ordinary fragrance bottles.

## In short

Storage means: dark, cool, dry, closed and out of reach.

## Personal guidance

If you are unsure whether a use fits your home, ask Kyle. Especially around children, pets, sensitive skin, pregnancy, medication or small rooms, calm guidance is more useful than spontaneous experimenting.
