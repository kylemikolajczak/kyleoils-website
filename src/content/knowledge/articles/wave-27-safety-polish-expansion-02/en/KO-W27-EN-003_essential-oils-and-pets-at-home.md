# Essential oils and pets at home

Pets perceive their environment differently from humans. That is why essential oils should be used especially cautiously and not casually in homes with animals.

## Allow an escape option

If you diffuse, an animal should be able to leave the room. Closed rooms without escape options are not a good idea.

## Less and shorter

Use fewer drops, short diffuser times and good ventilation. Watch behavior: restlessness, leaving, drooling, coughing, tiredness or unusual behavior are warning signs.

## No direct use without professional knowledge

Do not put oils on fur, paws, sleeping areas, toys or pet blankets. Direct use with pets does not belong in spontaneous DIY routines.

## Extra care with cats, small animals and birds

Cats, birds and small animals can be especially sensitive. Here, restraint matters more than scent preference.

## In short

With pets: never trap them, use very sparingly, observe behavior and avoid direct application.

## Personal guidance

If you are unsure whether a use fits your home, ask Kyle. Especially around children, pets, sensitive skin, pregnancy, medication or small rooms, calm guidance is more useful than spontaneous experimenting.

This page does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In case of discomfort, swallowing, strong reactions or uncertainty, seek professional help.
