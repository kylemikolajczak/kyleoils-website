# When essential oils may not be the right choice

Good guidance does not mean always recommending an oil. Sometimes the best decision is: no oil, less scent, a break or professional clarification.

## When scent becomes unpleasant

Pressure in the head, nausea, irritation, coughing, restlessness or the need to leave the room are signals. Do not push through. Stop, ventilate and observe.

## When people or animals are affected

Children, pets, pregnant people, older people, scent-sensitive visitors or people with medical questions change the decision. The room does not only belong to the person starting the diffuser.

## When skin reacts

Burning, redness, itching or irritation means: stop, do not add more, clean gently and seek professional guidance when unsure.

## When you want to solve a problem

Essential oils should not be used to self-treat medical issues or replace professional help.

## In short

Sometimes the safest use of essential oils is: pause, ventilate, use less or skip them.

## Personal guidance

If you are unsure whether a use fits your home, ask Kyle. Especially around children, pets, sensitive skin, pregnancy, medication or small rooms, calm guidance is more useful than spontaneous experimenting.

## Note

This page does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In case of discomfort, swallowing, strong reactions or uncertainty, seek professional help.
