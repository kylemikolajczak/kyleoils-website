# Citrus oils, sun exposure and photosensitivity

Citrus oils smell fresh and light, but when used topically they can involve an important safety topic: photosensitivity.

## What photosensitivity means

Photosensitivity means that skin may react more sensitively to sunlight or UV light after certain oil use. This does not apply to every oil the same way, but matters with certain citrus oils.

## Which oils to check

Pay special attention to product labels for citrus oils such as Bergamot, Lime, Lemon or Grapefruit. The exact context depends on the specific product and label guidance.

## Practical rule

If an oil may be photosensitive, do not use it casually on skin areas that will later be exposed to sun or UV light. Diffuser use is different from topical use.

## What to avoid

Avoid citrus-heavy DIY roll-ons before sun, tanning beds or long outdoor exposure. Do not use a blend if you do not know the oils included.

## In short

Citrus scent feels light. Citrus topical use needs label, dilution and sun awareness.

If you are unsure whether a use fits your home, ask Kyle. Especially around children, pets, sensitive skin, pregnancy, medication or small rooms, calm guidance is more useful than spontaneous experimenting.
