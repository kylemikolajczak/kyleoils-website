# Eyes, mucous membranes and essential oils

Essential oils are concentrated. Eyes, mucous membranes and very sensitive skin areas are not the place for experiments.

## Why these areas are sensitive

Eyes and mucous membranes react faster and more strongly than normal skin. Even small amounts can feel uncomfortable.

## Hands are a risk

Many problems do not happen intentionally, but through hands: touching oil, then rubbing an eye, touching the nose or inserting contact lenses.

## What to avoid

Do not use oils in eyes, ears, nose, mouth, intimate areas or on irritated skin. Do not leave oil residue on hands when touching children, pets, contact lenses or sensitive surfaces.

## If something happens

Do not panic-rub with water. Oil does not simply dissolve in water. For strong discomfort, swallowing, eye irritation or uncertainty, seek professional help or contact your local poison control or emergency service.

## In short

Oils do not belong in eyes or mucous membranes. Wash hands and keep distance from sensitive areas.

## Personal guidance

If you are unsure whether a use fits your home, ask Kyle. Especially around children, pets, sensitive skin, pregnancy, medication or small rooms, calm guidance is more useful than spontaneous experimenting.
