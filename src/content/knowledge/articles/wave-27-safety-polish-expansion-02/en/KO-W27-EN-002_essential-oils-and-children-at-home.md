# Essential oils and children at home

When children live in the home, the rules change. Essential oils do not belong in children’s hands and should not be treated like ordinary fragrance products.

## Storage comes first

Oils should be stored out of sight and reach of children. A beautiful oil box at eye level may look interesting to children, but it is not safe storage.

## Diffuse with restraint

If you diffuse, do it briefly, sparingly and in well-ventilated spaces. Children should be able to leave the room or not be exposed to scent continuously.

## Be especially careful with topical use

Children’s skin is more delicate. doTERRA states that essential oils should always be diluted before topical use on children. Casual experiments with strong oils are not a good idea.

## What to avoid

Do not put oils on hands that may later touch eyes or mouth. Do not use strong oils without clear guidance. Do not present oils as solutions for children’s problems.

## In short

With children: store safely, use less, always dilute, observe carefully and skip use when unsure.

## Personal guidance

If you are unsure whether a use fits your home, ask Kyle. Especially around children, pets, sensitive skin, pregnancy, medication or small rooms, calm guidance is more useful than spontaneous experimenting.
