# Preventing skin reactions: amount, dilution and breaks

Many skin issues do not happen because an oil is “bad,” but because amount, dilution, frequency or context does not fit.

## Amount matters

More drops do not mean better use. With topical use, less is often more sensible, especially with new oils, sensitive skin and strong scent profiles.

## Dilution is not weakness

Dilution with a carrier oil makes use easier to control. doTERRA describes dilution as an important way to minimize skin sensitivity.

## Plan breaks

Not every routine needs to be daily or several times a day. If skin feels dry, irritated or sensitive, a break is often better than adding more.

## Do not skip patch testing

A patch test on a small area helps place a new oil or blend more cautiously. It is not a guarantee, but it is a useful step.

## In short

A more skin-considerate routine is smaller, more diluted, slower and better observed.

## Personal guidance

If you are unsure whether a use fits your home, ask Kyle. Especially around children, pets, sensitive skin, pregnancy, medication or small rooms, calm guidance is more useful than spontaneous experimenting.

## Note

This page does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In case of discomfort, swallowing, strong reactions or uncertainty, seek professional help.
