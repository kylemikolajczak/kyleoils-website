# 5-minute morning routine with essential oils

A morning routine does not need to be perfect. Five minutes are enough to begin the day more consciously: ventilate, choose a scent, orient yourself briefly and start with one small action.

## The routine

### Minute 1: Clear air and space

Open a window briefly and clear the visible area a little. Essential oils feel clearer as room scent when the space is not fighting stale air, kitchen smells or clutter.

### Minute 2: Choose one scent

Choose one oil or a simple two-oil blend. Morning routines often fit bright citrus notes, green herbal profiles or a clear wood-citrus combination.

### Minute 3: Start very lightly

Use only a few drops and consider room size, children, pets and scent sensitivity. A morning ritual should feel fresh, not dominate the whole room.

### Minute 4: Write one sentence

Write briefly: What matters today? What can I leave out? What is the next small step? This makes the scent an anchor, not only a pleasant extra.

### Minute 5: Begin

Turn the diffuser off after a short time or let it run only as long as it stays pleasant. The routine is complete when you move into the day with more clarity.

## Good oil profiles for this routine

Good starting profiles: Lemon, Grapefruit, Lime, Spearmint, Rosemary, Cedarwood or Cypress. Always use sparingly and follow the label.

## Important context

No performance, concentration or health promises. This routine is a scent and structure support for the morning.

## Safety

Always read the product label, start with less, dilute for topical use and consider children, pets, pregnancy, medication, sensitive skin and scent sensitivity. Essential oils do not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
