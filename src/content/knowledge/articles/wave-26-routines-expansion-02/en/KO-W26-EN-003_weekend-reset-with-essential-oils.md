# Weekend reset with essential oils

A weekend reset does not need to be a perfect self-care day. Often one hour with a clear sequence is enough: ventilate, sort, scent lightly, plan and begin again consciously.

## The routine

### Phase 1: Fresh air

Open windows, move air through the rooms and remove visible distractions. Scent should not fight chaos; it should set a new atmosphere afterward.

### Phase 2: One room, one scent

Choose one room instead of the whole home. Start with a fresh scent profile in the living area, kitchen or work area. Fewer rooms mean more clarity.

### Phase 3: Small order

Take 15 minutes for one visible surface: table, desk, kitchen counter or entryway. The routine works better when space and scent fit together.

### Phase 4: Weekly overview

Write down three things: What is open? What matters? What can be left out? The scent becomes linked with planning and calm, not distraction.

### Phase 5: Do not overextend

End the reset before it becomes a huge task. A small repeatable routine is more valuable than a perfect day you never repeat.

## Good oil profiles for this routine

Fresh starting profiles: Lemon, Grapefruit, Lime, Eucalyptus, Cypress, Siberian Fir or Cedarwood.

## Important context

No detox, cleansing or health promise. This is about room feel, order and conscious planning.

## Safety

Always read the product label, start with less, dilute for topical use and consider children, pets, pregnancy, medication, sensitive skin and scent sensitivity. Essential oils do not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
