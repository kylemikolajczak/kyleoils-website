# Starter routine for families

In families, an oil routine is not a solo project. Children, pets, visitors, room size and scent sensitivity all need to be considered. So the rule is: small, sparing, clear.

## The routine

### Rule 1: Adults decide

Essential oils stay out of reach of children. Adults decide what is used, how much, where and for how long.

### Rule 2: One room, short time

At the beginning, one well-ventilated room and a short diffuser time are enough. No constant scenting, no strong blends, no experiments in children’s rooms.

### Rule 3: Simple scent profiles

Start with gently perceived, well-understood scent profiles and very small amounts. Explore new oils one at a time.

### Rule 4: Be extra careful with topical use

With children, sensitive skin or uncertainty, do not use oils topically casually. If used at all, keep it label-aligned, strongly diluted and clearly understood.

### Rule 5: Observe pets

Animals can perceive scent differently. Always leave escape options and do not diffuse constantly in closed rooms.

## Good oil profiles for this routine

For families, the largest collection is usually not the best start. A very clear selection with safety rules is better.

## Important context

This routine does not replace individual guidance. Be especially cautious around children, pregnancy, pets or uncertainty.

## Safety

Always read the product label, start with less, dilute for topical use and consider children, pets, pregnancy, medication, sensitive skin and scent sensitivity. Essential oils do not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
