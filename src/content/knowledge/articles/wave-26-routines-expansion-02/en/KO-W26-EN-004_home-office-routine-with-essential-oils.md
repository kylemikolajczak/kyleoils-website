# Home office routine with essential oils

In a home office, living and working can blur quickly. A scent routine can help mark transitions more consciously: start, break and finish.

## The routine

### Start signal

Choose a clear, light scent for the start of work. Diffuse briefly and open your work window mentally: What is today’s most important task?

### Work phase

During work, scent should stay in the background. If you constantly notice it, it is probably too strong. A few drops and short times are often enough.

### Break marker

Use your break without scent: window open, water, short walk. This keeps scent as a signal rather than a constant cloud.

### Finish

At the end, a different, softer scent can help separate work from living space. Important: the routine symbolically ends work; it does not replace rest.

### Repetition

Use two stable scent profiles rather than something new every day. Repetition makes the routine easier to understand.

## Good oil profiles for this routine

Start: Lemon, Rosemary, Grapefruit, Spearmint. Finish: Cedarwood, Cypress, Roman Chamomile or soft wood notes.

## Important context

No concentration, productivity or performance promises. Here, scent is a room and transition signal.

## Safety

Always read the product label, start with less, dilute for topical use and consider children, pets, pregnancy, medication, sensitive skin and scent sensitivity. Essential oils do not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
