# Self-care routine after a long day

After a long day, you often do not need a big wellness ceremony. A small real routine is enough: change the room, scent lightly, notice your body and close the day consciously.

## The routine

### Change the room

Consciously step out of work or everyday mode. Change rooms, open a window or clear one small surface.

### Set scent lightly

Choose a scent profile that feels pleasant to you. Not the strongest, not the most exciting, but what truly fits today.

### Notice your body

Place a hand on your chest or belly, breathe normally and ask: What do I actually need now? Water, quiet, food, movement, conversation, stillness?

### Optional: diluted roll-on

If you use a skin-based routine, keep it diluted and label-aligned. Do not test new strong oils in the evening.

### Close

End the routine with one clear action: diffuser off, write a note, phone away, make tea or shower. Small, but conscious.

## Good oil profiles for this routine

Fitting scent profiles can include Cedarwood, Lavender, Roman Chamomile, Bergamot, Black Spruce or very soft blends.

## Important context

No healing, stress-reduction or relaxation promises. This routine supports conscious self-care, not medical treatment.

## Safety

Always read the product label, start with less, dilute for topical use and consider children, pets, pregnancy, medication, sensitive skin and scent sensitivity. Essential oils do not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
