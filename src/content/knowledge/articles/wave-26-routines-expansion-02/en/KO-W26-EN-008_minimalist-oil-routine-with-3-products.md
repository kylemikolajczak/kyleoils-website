# Minimalist oil routine with 3 products

You do not need many products to explore essential oils in a useful way. A minimalist routine with three products can often be better than a large cart without a plan.

## The routine

### Product 1: One room scent

Choose one oil or blend that you understand in the diffuser and truly like. This teaches you scent, room size, amount and your own perception.

### Product 2: One practical Touch or roll-on product

If topical use fits you, a ready-made Touch product or clearly diluted roll-on can be more practical than several open bottles.

### Product 3: One learning oil

Choose one single oil you want to understand consciously: scent family, botanical name, safety context, blending logic and everyday situation.

### Test for four weeks

Use these three products intentionally for four weeks. Note what actually gets used and what only sounded interesting.

### Only then expand

Expand only when you can see what is truly missing: another oil, a kit, a system or maybe nothing at all.

## Good oil profiles for this routine

This routine fits beginners, scent-sensitive people or anyone who wants to start doTERRA calmly without product overwhelm.

## Important context

Minimalist does not automatically mean risk-free. Even a few products require labels, dilution and conscious use.

## Safety

Always read the product label, start with less, dilute for topical use and consider children, pets, pregnancy, medication, sensitive skin and scent sensitivity. Essential oils do not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
