# Room scent routine for guests

When guests come over, less is almost always better. A good guest scent is not loud. It makes the room feel cared for without overwhelming visitors.

## The routine

### 60 minutes before: clear the room

Ventilate, remove visible clutter and decide which room actually needs scent. The whole house does not need fragrance.

### 30 minutes before: scent briefly

Use a very light blend and diffuse only briefly. Then the diffuser can stay off so only a subtle scent signature remains.

### On arrival: stay neutral

When guests are present, scent should not dominate. Do not make the scent a big topic; simply observe whether anyone seems sensitive.

### With sensitive guests: better without

Children, pregnant guests, pets, respiratory sensitivity or scent sensitivity are reasons to be extra careful. When unsure, ventilate instead of diffusing.

### After the visit

Tidy up, ventilate and use a fresh room scent later if needed. Do not keep adding more scent during the visit.

## Good oil profiles for this routine

Good guest scents: light citrus-wood blends, Grapefruit + Cedarwood, Lime + Cypress or Wild Orange + Cedarwood.

## Important context

There is no guarantee that every guest likes a scent. Guest scent should always stay subtle and considerate.

## Safety

Always read the product label, start with less, dilute for topical use and consider children, pets, pregnancy, medication, sensitive skin and scent sensitivity. Essential oils do not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
