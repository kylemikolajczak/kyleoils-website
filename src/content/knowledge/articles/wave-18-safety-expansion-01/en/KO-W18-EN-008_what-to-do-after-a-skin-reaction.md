# What to do after a skin reaction?

If your skin reacts after using an essential oil, this is not a moment to keep experimenting. Stopping, assessing and seeking help when needed matter more than pushing through.

## The simple answer

Stop use immediately. Remove the product as well as you can, avoid applying more oils to the area and observe the reaction. Seek medical guidance for strong, persistent or unusual symptoms.

## Possible signs

Possible reactions include burning, itching, redness, warmth, rash, swelling, blisters, dryness, headache, nausea or breathing difficulty. Breathing difficulty, strong swelling or circulation problems are emergency signs.

## What not to do

Do not keep applying oil. Do not try to “balance it out” with other essential oils. Do not increase the amount. Do not ignore a reaction that gets stronger.

## Why water may not be enough

Essential oils are oil-soluble. Water alone may sometimes spread them rather than remove them well. A mild carrier oil or suitable cleansing product may be appropriate depending on the situation. Ask qualified guidance when unsure.

## In short

A skin reaction is a stop signal. After that, do not experiment; assess carefully.
