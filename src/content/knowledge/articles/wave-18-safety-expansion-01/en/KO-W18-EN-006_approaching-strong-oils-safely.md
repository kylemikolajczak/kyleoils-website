# How to approach strong oils safely

Not every essential oil feels the same. Some oils are much more intense in scent, on skin or in blends and need extra respect.

## The simple answer

Strong oils should not be used spontaneously, undiluted or in large amounts by beginners. Less is better, and the product label matters more than a social media tip.

## How to recognize strong oils

Strong oils are often sharp, spicy, minty, herbal or very dominant in a diffuser. Depending on the product, examples can include Oregano, Cinnamon, Clove, Peppermint, Rosemary, Eucalyptus or Lemongrass.

## Why caution matters

Strong oils can irritate skin and mucous membranes, become very present in a diffuser or be too much around children, pets and scent-sensitive people.

## A beginner-safe rule

Use strong oils sparingly, dilute for topical use, diffuse briefly and avoid eyes, nose and mucous membranes. If children, pets, pregnancy, medication or medical conditions are involved, clarify first.

## In short

Strong oils are not bad. They simply need clear boundaries.
