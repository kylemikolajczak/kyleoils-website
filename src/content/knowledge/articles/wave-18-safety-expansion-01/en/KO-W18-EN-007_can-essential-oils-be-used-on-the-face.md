# Can essential oils be used on the face?

The face is one of the most sensitive areas for essential oils. Eyes, nose, lips, mucous membranes and thinner skin are very close together.

## The simple answer

For beginners, facial use is not a good first step. If considered at all, it should be product-specific, highly diluted, very limited, away from the eyes and approached with extra care.

## Why the face is sensitive

Facial skin can react more quickly. Scent components or oils can also move close to eyes or mucous membranes, which can be uncomfortable or problematic.

## What to avoid

Avoid oils near eyes, nose, lips, mucous membranes, irritated skin, open areas, sunburned skin or freshly treated skin. Do not use strong oils on the face and avoid DIY recipes without clear guidance.

## A better starting point

If you want to learn essential oils, start with diffuser basics, dilution on less sensitive skin areas or simple scent routines away from the face.

## In short

Facial use requires more caution than many other routines. Not everything natural belongs on the face.
