# Essential oils and breastfeeding

Breastfeeding is a particularly sensitive part of everyday life because scent, skin contact and close contact with a baby come together. Essential oils should not be used casually in this context.

## The simple answer

During breastfeeding, use essential oils very cautiously and seek qualified guidance when unsure. This is especially important for topical use near the chest, strong scents, internal use and oils close to the baby.

## Why scent matters differently here

A baby is very close to your skin and can experience scent intensely. What feels pleasant to you may be too strong for a baby. Skin contact can also transfer products to places where they should not be.

## What matters most

Avoid essential oils on the breast area before nursing. Do not use strong oils directly around a baby. Keep rooms ventilated, diffuse briefly and stop if you or your baby show discomfort.

## What to avoid

Avoid internal use without medical guidance, oils on a baby’s skin, oils on your hands before direct contact, strong diffuser blends in small rooms and any attempt to treat symptoms on your own.

## In short

During breastfeeding: keep distance, stay conservative, read labels and ask qualified professionals when unsure.
