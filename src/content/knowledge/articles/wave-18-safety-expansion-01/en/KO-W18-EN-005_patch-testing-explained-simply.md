# Patch testing explained simply

A patch test is a small skin-tolerance test. It can help you start more carefully, but it is not a guarantee and does not replace medical diagnosis.

## The simple answer

A patch test means testing a product on a very small area before using it in a larger routine. With essential oils, this should usually be done diluted.

## When a patch test helps

It is especially useful for sensitive skin, new oils, new blends, topical routines, scented products or if you have reacted to cosmetics before.

## How to think carefully

Do not test several new things at once. Choose a small area, use a small amount, avoid irritated skin and observe for redness, itching, burning or discomfort.

## Limits of patch testing

A normal patch test does not mean every future use is safe. Amount, skin area, frequency, sun exposure, skin condition and combinations can all matter.

## In short

A patch test is a safety filter, not a safety guarantee.
