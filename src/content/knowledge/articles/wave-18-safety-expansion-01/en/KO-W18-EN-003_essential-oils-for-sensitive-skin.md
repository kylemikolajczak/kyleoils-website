# Essential oils for sensitive skin

Sensitive skin needs more caution. Essential oils are concentrated and may cause irritation or allergic reactions in some people.

## The simple answer

If you have sensitive skin, do not test essential oils undiluted or over large areas. Start small, diluted, one product at a time and observe carefully.

## Why sensitive skin can react differently

Sensitive skin may respond more quickly to fragrance, plant components, cosmetic products or essential oils. An oil that works for someone else may still be too intense for you.

## A safer start

Use a carrier oil, choose a low dilution, test only a small skin area and wait. Avoid introducing several new products at once so you can understand what caused a reaction.

## Stop-rules

Stop use if you notice burning, itching, redness, swelling, rash, breathing difficulty, discomfort or an unusual reaction. Seek qualified guidance if symptoms are strong or continue.

## In short

Sensitive skin does not automatically mean essential oils are impossible. It means slower, smaller, more diluted and more conscious.
