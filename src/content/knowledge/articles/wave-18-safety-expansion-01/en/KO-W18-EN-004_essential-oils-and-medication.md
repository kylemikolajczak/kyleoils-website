# Essential oils and medication

When medication is involved, essential oil use should be considered with extra care. This is not only about internal use, but also frequent or large-area topical use.

## The simple answer

If you take medication or have a medical condition, do not use essential oils as a self-experiment. Clarify uncertain uses with a qualified healthcare professional.

## Why this matters

Medication, medical conditions, pregnancy, liver or kidney concerns, skin conditions, allergies and other personal factors can influence what may be appropriate or problematic.

## Especially sensitive areas

Internal use, strong oils, frequent daily use, large-area topical use and products near mucous membranes need extra caution.

## What to avoid

Avoid the idea that “natural is always safe.” Do not replace medication, change dosages or try to treat symptoms with oils on your own.

## In short

Essential oils can be part of a personal routine, but they do not replace medical treatment and should not be used around medication questions without professional guidance.
