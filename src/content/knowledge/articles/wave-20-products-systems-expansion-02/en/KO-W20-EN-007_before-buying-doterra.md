# What should I know before buying doTERRA?

Before buying doTERRA, a short overview helps. Not to slow you down, but to make your decision clearer, safer and more useful.

## 1. A product is not yet a routine

A product in the cart is not the same as everyday use. Before buying, ask: When will I use it? Where will it live? How often? With which safety context?

## 2. Quality does not replace safe use

doTERRA strongly positions itself around quality and product standards. Still, even high-quality essential oils are concentrated and require labels, dilution and conscious use.

## 3. Understand purchasing advantages properly

A customer account or purchasing advantage can make sense if you plan to order regularly. It should not be the reason to buy more than you understand or will use.

## 4. Systems need sequence

Wellness Made Simple, MetaPWR or other product systems can provide orientation. For beginners, the important point is not starting everything at once, but understanding the structure.

## In short

Before buying doTERRA, clarity, safety and everyday fit matter more than a full cart.

## What you can do with this

Before deciding, write down three things: What do I actually want to use in everyday life? Which safety questions matter? What would be a small, useful first step?
