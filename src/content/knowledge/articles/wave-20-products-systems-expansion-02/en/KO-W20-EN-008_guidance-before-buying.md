# Why guidance before buying makes sense

Guidance before buying is not a sales detour. It is often the fastest way to make a better decision.

## Why guidance helps

doTERRA has many products, kits, systems and account options. That is both a strength and a challenge. Guidance helps turn many options into a fitting sequence.

## What good guidance clarifies

Good guidance does not only ask which product sounds interesting. It looks at everyday life, budget, scent sensitivity, safety, children, pets, skin, previous experience, expectations and useful next steps.

## What guidance can prevent

Guidance can help prevent starting too big, misjudging products, opening too many oils at once, missing safety questions or buying a kit that does not fit your life.

## What guidance is not

Guidance is not a medical diagnosis, not a medical promise and not purchase pressure. With Kyle Oils, the goal is that you understand what you do and why you do it.

## In short

Guidance before buying does not make doTERRA more complicated. It makes the start clearer, calmer and more honest.

## What you can do with this

Before deciding, write down three things: What do I actually want to use in everyday life? Which safety questions matter? What would be a small, useful first step?

## Personal guidance

If you are unsure which product, kit or system fits you, ask Kyle. Together, you can place your first decision into a calm, honest and no-pressure context.
