# How to plan your first order

A good first order is not the biggest order. A good first order is one you understand, will use and can safely build into everyday life.

## Step 1: Clarify the goal

Before choosing products, clarify your starting point: room scent, simple routine, Oil Library, starter kit, product system or long-term reordering. Without a goal, the shop can quickly feel large and confusing.

## Step 2: Check safety context

Children, pets, sensitive skin, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medication, scent sensitivity and internal use are not side issues. They help determine which products fit and which should be left aside at first.

## Step 3: Start small enough

Do not only plan what sounds interesting. Plan what you will actually use in the next four weeks. A diffuser start, single oil, blend, Touch product or clear kit can be enough.

## Step 4: Understand account and advantages

A doTERRA customer account or possible purchasing advantages can make sense if you truly want to order. They do not replace product orientation. Understand first, order second.

## In short

The best first order is clear, safe and everyday-friendly. Not everything that sounds interesting needs to go into the cart.

## What you can do with this

Before deciding, write down three things: What do I actually want to use in everyday life? Which safety questions matter? What would be a small, useful first step?

## Personal guidance
