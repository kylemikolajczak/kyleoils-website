# How to build a simple monthly routine

A monthly routine helps you not just try doTERRA once, but place it into everyday life more clearly. The focus is repetition, not more products.

## Why monthly thinking helps

A month is long enough to observe a routine, but short enough to keep it simple. You can see what you actually use, what stays unused and what should be planned better next time.

## A simple structure

Choose one room-scent moment, one personal routine and one reorder or learning note. That is enough at the beginning. For example: morning diffuser, one diluted roll-on moment and a month-end check of what was truly used.

## Do not reorder blindly

Reordering should come from use, not from FOMO. If a product runs out because you used it regularly and meaningfully, that is a different signal than a spontaneous cart.

## Where loyalty orders fit

A loyalty or monthly order setup can be practical when your routine is stable. At the beginning, it matters more to know which products truly belong in your life.

## In short

A monthly routine makes doTERRA calmer, more planable and less chaotic. Small repetition beats large product lists.

## What you can do with this

Before deciding, write down three things: What do I actually want to use in everyday life? Which safety questions matter? What would be a small, useful first step?
