# Tangerine

## Calm overview

Tangerine is a sweet, fruity citrus oil that doTERRA Europe describes as a flavour addition for foods and drinks. It works as a simple beginner profile for people who enjoy citrus oils.

## Scent profile

- easy to describe calmly
- useful for people who do not want to choose oils by name only
- a good guidance profile because use context and safety matter

## Use context

This profile stays intentionally general. The current doTERRA product label matters first.

For kitchen or food-flavouring contexts, it is especially important to remember that essential oils are highly concentrated and are not dosed like normal spices.

For Touch or topical products, skin feel, skin response and sensitive areas need to be considered.

## Safety and label

doTERRA Europe also provides clear safety notes for Tangerine: use only diluted, no more than one drop per day, avoid eyes, face, sensitive areas and mucosa, and after skin contact avoid sunlight and UV rays for at least 12 hours.
