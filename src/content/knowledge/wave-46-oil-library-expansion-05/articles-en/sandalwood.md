# Sandalwood

## Calm overview

Sandalwood is a warm, woody oil with a calm scent profile. doTERRA Europe describes it in the context of meditation, mood and skincare. For Kyle Oils, it fits as a premium wood oil that benefits from clear explanation.

## Use context

This profile stays intentionally general. The current doTERRA product label matters first.

For kitchen or food-flavouring contexts, it is especially important to remember that essential oils are highly concentrated and are not dosed like normal spices.

For Touch or topical products, skin feel, skin response and sensitive areas need to be considered.

## Safety and label

doTERRA Europe lists Sandalwood for topical use, massage, bath and perfuming with dilution guidance. As always: read the label, avoid sensitive areas and start carefully with sensitive skin.
